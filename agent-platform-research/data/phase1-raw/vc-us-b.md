## Notes
- Only Menlo, Bessemer, and Battery expose crawlable full portfolio pages; Insight, Index, Coatue, General Catalyst, and NEA were swept via their investment announcement blogs and targeted funding searches, so their holdings here are almost certainly undercounted.
- Customer service is the densest vertical (Cognigy, Ada, Decagon, Parloa, Observe.AI, Level AI, Neuron7, Wonderful), followed by legal (Harvey, EvenUp, Eve, Legora, Manifest, Flank) and security agents (7AI, Crogl, Legion, Reco, Armadin, RunSybil); software engineering agents now span open source (OpenHands), IDEs (Windsurf), and platforms (Factory, Tessl, Reflection).
- Agent infrastructure (L1) deals cluster at seed and Series A (E2B, Tavily, Dome, Engram, Kite, AIMon, Recall); Arize is the only L1 name at Series C scale, suggesting infra winners have not yet separated.
- Consolidation is already heavy: Aisera to Automation Anywhere, Windsurf to Cognition, Sana to Workday, SmarterDx to New Mountain Capital, TruEra to Snowflake, plus Menlo exits Fintool (Microsoft) and Graphite (Cursor).
- An emerging L4 pattern shows VCs funding outcome-based agent delivery (Unframe, $100M TCV in twelve months) and AI-enabled services rollups (Titan), blurring the product versus services line.
- Roughly twenty small Bessemer and Battery AI portfolio companies (e.g. Act Security, Aivar, Breaker, Modelcode, Kovr.ai, Tenzai, Bench IQ) had insufficient public information to classify and were left out; a few may prove agentic.
