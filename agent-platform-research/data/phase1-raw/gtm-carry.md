## Notes
- 39 of 85 prior-dataset companies survive the agentic-primary screen; the GTM dataset was workflow-heavy by design, so most exclusions are RFP, CPQ, PSA, and CS suites adding AI features rather than agent platforms
- The densest agentic cluster is RFP and proposal automation (10 of 39), split between commercial (AutogenAI, Arphie, Tribble) and government contracting (GovDash, Vultron, Procurement Sciences)
- Six incumbent-suite agent platforms carry over as L5; their agent offerings are now core positioning, not features
- 11x is the cautionary tale the census should keep visible: category-defining AI SDR with a public implosion
- Copy.ai's merger into Fullcast and Vivun's pivot to AI teammates are the two clearest pivots in the set
