## Notes
- Software engineering and customer service dominate late-stage agent valuations: Cognition ($48B), Anysphere (raising at ~$50B), Sierra ($15B+), plus Moveworks and Console exiting to ServiceNow and Palo Alto Networks.
- Security operations is the most common early-stage wedge: Dropzone AI, EdgeRunner, Impart, Artemis, Horizon3.ai, Norm Ai, Daylight Security and Corridor span the Madrona, Craft, Felicis and 8VC portfolios.
- A distinct agent-infrastructure layer is forming at seed and Series A: memory (Letta), governance (Runlayer), agent payments and identity (Nekuda, Kite AI), observability and evals (WhyLabs, TruEra), browser access (Browser Use).
- Many headline AI holdings failed the agentic screen: model providers (OpenAI, Cerebras, Together), AI search (Perplexity, OpenEvidence), content generation (Suno, Runway, HeyGen), AI-feature SaaS (Klarna, Ramp, Notion, Grammarly, DeepL).
- Consolidation is already significant: Moveworks to ServiceNow, TruEra to Snowflake, Forethought to Zendesk, Console to Palo Alto Networks, Sana to Workday.
- Attribution required verification: several assumed relationships did not check out (ElevenLabs is not Felicis- or Thrive-backed; Cognition is not an ICONIQ or Thrive holding; Mercor's lead investor is Felicis), and OpenAGI's "Lux" is a model name, not the firm.
