## Notes
- YC's 2026 structure: official batches Winter 2026 (Demo Day ~Mar 2026, ~199 companies) and Summer 2026 (Demo Day late Aug-early Sep 2026, ~236-241 companies); a Fall 2026 batch is already running and the open-source directory mirror also references a small Spring 2026 batch, so YC now runs more than two cycles per year.
- Agent density: 108 of 342 screened companies (~32%) passed the agentic screen — roughly 24% of W26 (45/190) and 44% of S26 screened (63/142); S26 coverage skews AI because press coverage of that batch focused on agents (one analysis was titled 'AI agents became the customer').
- Verticals: healthcare (Patientdesk.ai, Beacon Health, Eos AI, ClaimGlide, Overdrive Health), legal (Erinys, General Legal, Osmaura, Locke), finance/accounting (Rex, Zomma, Last Accounting Company, Levocred), logistics (Derya, Peer), and real estate/construction (RealPact, Atlia, Vestris, Verdant) are the densest clusters; Extruct counted 56 W26 companies (28%) as 'AI-native service' — agents doing the job end-to-end.
- Agent infrastructure (L1) is already crowded at pre-seed: agent control planes (Agentic Fabriq), observability/evals (Archal, Agnost AI, Buildbox, Akon Labs, Codag), agent identity/security (Hex Security, Agentcard), voice-agent infra (Dialogus), and MCP/browser tooling (Manufact — batch unconfirmed; KERNEL and Aside are Summer 2025, outside the swept batches).
- Traction is unusually early even for YC: Hex Security crossed $1M ARR within 8 weeks of launch, Luel neared $2M ARR in 6 weeks, and the standard W26 round was reported at $4M on a $40M post-money valuation.
- Coverage caveat: W26 enumeration reached ~95% of the batch (190/199 names); S26 reached ~60% (142/236) because the YC directory is client-rendered and the open-source mirror lacks a summer-2026 file; the S26 gap concentrates in non-AI companies (hardware, biotech, consumer), so agentic coverage is likely near-complete.
