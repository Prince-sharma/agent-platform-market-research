## Notes
- Every major incumbent suite now ships a first-party agent offering; the dominant pattern is a low-code agent studio plus prebuilt role agents (Copilot Studio, Agentforce, Oracle AI Agent Studio, Joule, Zia Agent Studio, Illuminate, Freddy).
- Product churn is unusually high for enterprise software: Breeze renamed Agent Hub, Agentspace folded into Gemini Enterprise, Vertex AI renamed Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform, Bedrock Agents demoted to Classic, Q Business closed in favor of Amazon Quick, and Q Developer is giving way to Kiro.
- RPA and process incumbents (UiPath, Automation Anywhere, Pega, Celonis) have repositioned their core narrative around agentic automation; UiPath shows named enterprise agentic wins.
- Security and developer suites are rolling out agentic products (Security Copilot, Cortex AgentiX, Charlotte AI; GitHub, GitLab, JetBrains coding agents) but rarely publish adoption numbers.
- Vertical suites (legal, insurance, construction, healthcare, life sciences, manufacturing) announced agents almost universally in 2025-2026; published adoption evidence is thin, mostly launch and GA announcements.
- Several seed items are L1/L2 platforms rather than suite agents (AgentCore, Strands, ADK, Azure AI Foundry, Mosaic Agent Framework); rows are classified by what the product actually is.
