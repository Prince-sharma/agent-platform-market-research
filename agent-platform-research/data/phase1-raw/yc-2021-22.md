## Notes
- These batches predate the agent wave: most "agent" companies are 2024-26 repositionings of workflow-automation, RPA, or data companies (Peakflo, Activepieces, OpenCode ex-SST, Datrics, Shaped), and their YC directory descriptions now carry agent language.
- Voice is the dominant cross-cutting modality (14 entries), concentrated in healthcare RCM, collections, and insurance: Vapi, Vogent, Feather, SigmaMind AI, Infer, Sequin, Opkit, Argovox, AviaryAI, Solum Health, Delfino AI, Flair Labs, Aurelian.
- Healthcare is the deepest vertical (about 20 entries across the four batches): Triomics, Camber, Sully.ai, Rejoy, Datrics, Ankr Health, Arintra, Splendor, Sophys, Opkit, Argovox, Solum Health, Delfino AI — prior-auth, claims coding, intake, and phone calls are the recurring wedge.
- GTM/sales agents form the largest single cluster, especially in S22 and W22: Champ, Boundo, Ciro, Avina, Popsy, Superbar, Truva, Penguin AI, RocketSDR, Sprites, Kular.
- Agent infrastructure (L1) is thin in these batches — Exa, Hyperbrowser, Bytebot (acquired), Windmill, BotCity, Openlayer, idemeum, Miniloop, Lamin, Shaped, Beam, Stably AI — the infra layer arrived with the 2023+ batches.
- Directory mirrors and live YC pages disagree for several companies (AXAR AI team 41 vs 4, Atonomo now Bunting Labs GIS models, Boundo now a motion-design agent, Lovage now slot-filling marketing); live YC pages were treated as authoritative, which demoted several screener CLEARs to borderline.
