## Notes
- F23 does not exist: YC ran two 2023 batches (W23: 282 companies; S23: 229 companies). The first Fall batch was F24, announced August 2024 with Demo Day in December 2024. This file therefore covers W23 and S23 only.
- S23 was the agent wave's inflection point: B2B was roughly 70% of the batch and AI-native automation dominated; W23 skewed more toward thin "AI features" wrappers, several of which later pivoted.
- Voice agents were the densest cross-cutting modality: at least 12 companies across both batches (Bland, Vocode, Sameday, Avoca, Prosper, Remy, Strada, Health Harbor, Wattson, Retape, Twine, Pierre), concentrated in healthcare, insurance, and home services.
- Healthcare back-office is the densest vertical cluster (Tennr, Mantys, Cair, Shasta, Prosper, Remy, Wattson, Health Harbor, Decoda): eligibility verification, claims, prior auth, and referrals.
- Outcomes by 2026 are sharply bimodal: Tennr ($101M Series C), Bland ($50M+), Giga ($61M Series A reported), HappyRobot ($44M Series B), Prosper ($30M a16z), plus Automat, Escape, Pylon, Hadrius, Greenlite raised significant follow-ons; Flowise was acquired by Workday; most others show no visible follow-on. Notable pivots: Rivet (game servers to agent infrastructure), dili (PE diligence to construction compliance), Sweep (GitHub PRs to JetBrains), Reworkd (AgentGPT to web scraping).
- Agent infrastructure (L1) was scarce in 2023: only Atla, Langfuse, Parea, CORE, OpenTools, Credal, and Rivet; the L1 layer consolidated around later batches while 2023 favored vertical agents.
