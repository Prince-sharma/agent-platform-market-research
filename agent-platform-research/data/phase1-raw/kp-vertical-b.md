## Notes
- Vertical depth is uneven: legal (16), healthcare (20), and GTM (17) each support many funded pure-plays, while construction (5) and logistics (5) stay thin; another ~30 seed-stage names surfaced below confirmation grade (Krane, Conxai, Neuron Factory, 5U AI, Freight Hero, Cargofy, Huscarl, FurtherAI, Magnar, Moritz, Panta, and others).
- 2026 is a mega-round year for vertical agents: Harvey at $11B (reportedly seeking $15.5B), Abridge at $5.3B with $100M+ ARR, Clay reportedly near $7B, Legora raising $600M, EliseAI in talks at $3.7B, Forus at $3B, plus $1B+ marks for Eve, Rillet, Norm Ai, HappyRobot, Assort Health, Hippocratic AI, and Rox.
- Voice is the dominant agent modality in logistics (HappyRobot, FleetWorks) and healthcare patient access (Assort Health, Corti), and has reached collections (Salient at a $500M valuation).
- The L3/L5 boundary is blurring: incumbent suites (Workiva, BlackLine, Procore, Outreach) now ship agent features; I classified the two suite seeds (Workiva, BlackLine) as L5 rather than L3.
- Accounting is the most active extension vertical: 15 of 77 extension finds are 2025-26 finance agents (Maxima $41M, Stacks $23M, Capsa $18M, Quanta, Docyt, Zalos, Eagl, Finto, Neno, Numos, Maximor, Zenskar, Sapien, Uplinq, Unique), overwhelmingly seed-stage.
- Several seeds show no 2025-26 traction evidence (Vic.ai, Truewind, Unit21, Tribble, Regie, Henry AI, Zuma) and were marked normal priority; Cofactor was reclassified from the finance seed grouping to healthcare because its product fights hospital claim denials.
