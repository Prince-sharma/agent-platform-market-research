## Notes
- Vertical work agents dominate these portfolios: roughly two-thirds of qualifying companies are L3, with security (RunSybil, Exaforce, AirMDR, Horizon3.ai), finance and accounting (Hebbia, OffDeal, Octagon AI, Farsight, Basis), healthcare (Tennr, Qventus, Affineon Health, SuperDial), and legal (Harvey, Supio, Ruli AI) the densest clusters.
- Horizontal agent infrastructure is comparatively rare: the main L1 bets are Temporal (durable execution), Arize AI (observability and evals), Bespoke Labs (agent environments), SkyFireAI (agent payments), Solid (agent reliability), and Moonbounce (agent control); CopilotKit and Sema4.ai are the notable L2 platforms.
- 2025-2026 funding bifurcated sharply: mega-rounds for proven agent companies (Sierra at $15.8B, Cognition at $48B, Harvey at $11B, Horizon3.ai at $2B+) alongside a long tail of sub-$10M seed agent startups with no visible traction.
- Model providers with agent platforms (Cohere North, Mistral) were kept in Borderline since they remain model-first businesses despite agentic positioning, as did voice model providers (Cartesia, Ultravox, Gradium).
- Coverage gaps: Foundation Capital's portfolio page is lazy-loaded and its AI directory was unreachable, so its sweep relies on partial page data plus press; Amplify's list came from a third-party directory showing 28 of roughly 135 companies; several AI Fund and Radical portfolio names (Bhuma, Pixi, PuppyDog, Ribbon, Signal-1, Attuned Intelligence, others) could not be identified from public data and were excluded rather than guessed.
- Mayfield's agent franchise is fresh: its qualifying agent companies (Exaforce, Bespoke Labs, Hang Ten Systems, Basis) are 2025-2026 vintage, and its current portfolio pages emphasize newer AI-first bets over its historical enterprise roster.
