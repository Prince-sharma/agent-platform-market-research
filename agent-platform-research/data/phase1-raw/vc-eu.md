## Notes
- Voice agents command the largest rounds: Parloa's $350M Series D at a $3B valuation (Jan 2026) is the biggest agent outcome across these seven portfolios, and voice coverage spans EQT Ventures (Parloa), Point Nine (Calldesk) and Cherry (telli).
- Agent security and governance is the strongest emerging L1 theme, backed by four of the seven firms: Geordie (Balderton), Manifold Security (Cherry), Qevlar AI (EQT Ventures) and XBOW (Northzone) — a European bet on the agent control plane.
- The portfolios skew heavily to vertical work agents (roughly 70% of entries), led by legal (GC AI, Lawhive, Robin AI, DeepIP, Qura), security and finance; horizontal build platforms are rare, with deepset and Dust the clearest L2s.
- Northzone's agent cluster is unusually US-heavy (Blitzy, XBOW, GC AI), reflecting its multi-stage model, while Cherry Ventures and Seedcamp seed European vertical agents at pre-seed and seed stage.
- Exits have begun: Sana ($1.1B exit, Dec 2025), Re:infer (acquired by UiPath) and Speechly (acquired by Roblox) show incumbents buying European agent technology.
- Plural's deep-tech mandate yields few software-agent companies; its AI bets favor defense and infrastructure, with Robin AI the main pure agent play.
