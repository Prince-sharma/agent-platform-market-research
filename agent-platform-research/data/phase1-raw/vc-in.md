## Notes
- Voice AI is India's deepest agent cluster: GreyLabs, Ringg, Bolna, Mihup, Futwork, Agara, Persistence AI and Confido Health all sell voice agents, concentrated in BFSI collections, contact centers and US healthcare operations.
- Agent infrastructure is the fastest-emerging layer across these portfolios: Composio ($25M), Portkey ($15M), Maxim, Scalekit, Hyperbrowser and Zingle cover tool access, AI gateways, evals, agent authentication and browser runtime for agents.
- A "build from India for US verticals" pattern dominates Blume and Z47 portfolios: Coral, Confido Health, SwishX, Clientell, SiftHub and Prodigal target US healthcare, pharma and RevOps workflows with Indian engineering.
- Peak XV's 2025-2026 cadence (Surge 11 with 12 AI startups, five agentic bets at the February 2026 Impact AI PitchFest, 80+ AI investments claimed) marks a shift from consumer AI toward agent-driven work products; Elevation closed a $500M Fund IX in July 2026 aimed at the AI app layer.
- Z47's portfolio page lists several AI companies whose names do not render in text extraction (a DeVC-backed medical-coding agent firm, US-healthcare front-office agents, a voice foundation model company); DeVC is Matrix-anchored and writes $50K-$500K checks, so these are minor positions.
- Data quality caveat: the VCBacked directory mislabeled KushoAI (actually Antler India-backed) as a Blume investment; investor attributions in this file were re-verified against funding announcements wherever possible.
