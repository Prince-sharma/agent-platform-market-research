# Semiotic

**Thesis (one line):** AI design agents producing studio-grade landing pages at scale, replacing traditional high-cost agencies with a human-AI hybrid model.

- HQ: San Francisco
- YC batch: F25

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** drag-and-drop canvas and natural language prompting
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** MCP (coming soon)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** unknown
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** approval gates; human-AI hybrid with human judgment layer for briefs and feedback
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** unknown
7. **Pricing model:** platform fee (monthly subscription)
8. **GTM motion:** services-led (AI design agency) and PLG self-serve
9. **Moat thesis:** workflow lock-in and human-AI hybrid execution
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** multi-step workflow (minutes)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Agents generate design mockups, self-evaluate across devices/screen sizes, and refine landing pages based on human-curated briefs.
- **Weaknesses:** Heavy reliance on human intervention for initial client briefing and final quality control.
- **Trajectory:** Moving from a hybrid agency model toward a scalable AI design platform with integrated coding agents and custom LoRA training.
- **M&A:** none visible
