# Luthor

**Thesis (one line):** AI-native compliance infrastructure that automates regulatory review and governance for enterprise marketing in highly regulated industries.

## Facts

- Founded: 2024
- HQ: San Francisco
- YC batch: F24
- Backers: Garry Tan
- Funding: $500K Seed
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Regulated financial services firms (RIAs, broker-dealers, banks), mortgage lenders, and marketing agencies.
- Pricing model: unknown

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Upload-based review and centralized platform for review, approval, and audit.
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS.
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Rapid integration ("days, not months") into existing marketing workflows.
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Policy-driven review logic using federal, state, and international regulations, brand guidelines, and past approvals.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Approval gates; AI handles volume and pattern detection while humans retain final judgment and approval.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** SOC 2 Type II, AES-256 encryption, GDPR compliance, SEC 17a-4 immutable archival records, and audit trails.
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led enterprise motion targeting regulated sectors.
9. **Moat thesis:** Workflow lock-in via immutable audit trails and specialized regulatory rule sets (SEC, FINRA, FCA).
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary.
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow (minutes to hours) reducing review cycles from weeks to hours.

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Automates first-pass screening of ads, emails, and social posts; flags performance claims and missing disclosures; provides actionable fixes.
- **Weaknesses:** High dependency on the accuracy of regulatory rule mapping; limited visibility into pricing/commercial scalability.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from financial services into broader regulated marketing (e.g., piloting with Am Law 10 firms).
- **M&A:** none visible
