# Salesforce Agentforce

**Thesis (one line):** An L5 incumbent-suite agent platform integrating AI agents across CRM workflows using a consumption-based "Flex Credit" action model.

## Facts

- Founded: 1999
- HQ: San Francisco
- YC batch: none
- Backers: public company (CRM)
- Funding: public company (CRM)
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: $800M AI ARR (Sept 2026, high confidence)
- ICP: Enterprise customers of Salesforce CRM (Sales, Service, Marketing, IT, HR)
- Pricing model: Flex Credits ($0.10 per action) or per-user licenses ($125/user/month)

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** drag-and-drop canvas (Agentforce Builder) and natural language (Agent Script)
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** proprietary connector library (expanding to Gmail, Outlook, Dropbox in 2026)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** live sync with systems of record (Salesforce CRM objects)
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** approval gates; reasoning models (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini)
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC, audit trails, Digital Wallet for spend caps
7. **Pricing model:** platform fee plus usage (Flex Credits per action or per-conversation)
8. **GTM motion:** sales-led; bundled into existing CRM suite installed base
9. **Moat thesis:** data gravity, distribution, workflow lock-in
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** multi-step workflow (minutes) to persistent (always-on)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end automation of CRM tasks including updating records, resolving cases, and proactive cross-functional workflows.
- **Weaknesses:** High dependency on Salesforce ecosystem; potential ROI struggle for enterprises in early pilots.
- **Trajectory:** Moving toward "Agentic Enterprise" where agents are embedded in all employee workflows via Slack and CRM.
- **M&A:** Acquiring Fin (formerly Intercom) for ~$3.6B, announced June 15, 2026; expected to close in Q4 of Salesforce FY2027.
