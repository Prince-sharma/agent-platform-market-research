# Convexia

**Thesis (one line):** An AI-native pharma operator using specialized agents to autonomously source, evaluate, and manage the clinical development of drug assets.

## Facts

- Founded: 2025
- HQ: unknown
- YC batch: S25
- Backers: unknown
- Funding: unknown
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Small/mid pharma companies, Biotech VCs, and computational biotechs
- Pricing model: Tiered subscription and per-report fees ($199/mo, $1,497 per report, $2,997/mo Pro)

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Natural language / Playground (via convexia.bio/playground)
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary integration with comp bio models (ESM-3, RFdiffusion, Boltz-2, AlphaFold)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Live sync with public/private databases and unstructured global data; semi-supervised learning on biomedical data
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Approval gates; expert PhD and KOL review integrated into decision layers
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** unknown
7. **Pricing model:** Platform fee plus usage (subscription and per-report)
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led pilots with pharma/VCs; PLG elements for sourcing tools
9. **Moat thesis:** Data gravity (proprietary PoS model), workflow lock-in (end-to-end pharma stack), and specialized evals (ConvexiaBench)
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** Long-horizon (hours to weeks for diligence and trial planning)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end drug lifecycle management: scanning for overlooked assets, running in silico simulations (binding, toxicity, ADME/PK), market sizing, and simulating clinical trial execution risks (CRO/CMC).
- **Weaknesses:** High dependency on the accuracy of third-party computational biology models; significant regulatory hurdles for autonomous trial management.
- **Trajectory:** Transitioning from a SaaS-like pilot tool for BD teams to a full-stack "AI-maximalist" pharma company that owns and sells its own drug assets.
- **M&A:** none visible
