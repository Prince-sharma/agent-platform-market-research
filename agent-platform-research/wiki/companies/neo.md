# Neo

**Thesis (one line):** A real-time control layer providing inventory, posture intelligence, and policy enforcement to secure agentic AI across the enterprise.

## Facts

- Founded: 2025
- HQ: Boston
- YC batch: none
- Backers: Andreessen Horowitz (lead), Bessemer Venture Partners (lead), Craft Ventures, Merlin Ventures
- Funding: $100M total (July 2026), consisting of a $75M Series A and $25M Seed
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: SecOps teams in modern enterprises
- Pricing model: unknown

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Lightweight sensor for deployment with a management platform/dashboard for inventory and policy control.
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** unknown
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary sensor that monitors agents, plugins, and MCP servers across devices, browsers, and identities.
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Neoverse, a built-in knowledge base that continuously maps capabilities, risks, and behaviors of agentic software.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Enforces real-time policy controls and "verdicts" (e.g., blocking unauthorized credential reads).
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** Provides attribution, posture intelligence, and policy enforcement for human and non-human activity.
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led (scaling go-to-market and engineering teams following $100M raise).
9. **Moat thesis:** Data gravity via Neoverse artifact intelligence and deep integration into the agentic application layer.
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** Persistent (always-on real-time monitoring and control).

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Neo does not provide agent capabilities but enables agents to operate safely by governing their actions and access to sensitive data.
- **Weaknesses:** Operating in a highly crowded L1 agent security category with significant competition.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from stealth to scale rapidly, targeting the projected increase of agentic applications in the enterprise.
- **M&A:** none visible
