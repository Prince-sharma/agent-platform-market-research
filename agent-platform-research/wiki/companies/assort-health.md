11. **Task horizon:** multi-step workflow (minutes) to persistent (always-on inbound/outbound)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end patient access automation including inbound concierge (scheduling, triage), proactive outbound (reactivation, care gap closure), automated intake/referrals, and staff co-piloting.
- **Weaknesses:** High dependency on EHR integration stability; potential friction in highly regulated specialty protocols not covered by existing datasets.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from a voice-first scheduling tool to a full "Patient Journey" orchestration platform covering billing and clinical follow-ups.
- **M&A:** none visible
