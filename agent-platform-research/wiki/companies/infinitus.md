# Infinitus

**Thesis (one line):** Voice AI agents automating complex healthcare administrative phone calls for benefit verification, prior authorization, and patient navigation.

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Portal and API for bulk submission; unknown for agent configuration.
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS.
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary connector library; integrates with data providers, payors, and pharmacies.
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Built-in RAG leveraging an internal knowledge base from millions of calls and external data providers.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Fully autonomous voice agents with escalation to human operators if needed.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** unknown
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led.
9. **Moat thesis:** Data gravity (millions of calls), deep vertical expertise in 1,000+ therapies, and established payor relationships.
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary.
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow (minutes) for individual calls; bulk processing for large batches.

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end voice automation including IVR navigation, data collection (up to 150 points per call), pushing back on bad data, and handling complex healthcare workflows (benefit verification, PA status, appeals, and patient adherence).
- **Weaknesses:** High dependency on payor cooperation and the stability of legacy phone systems/IVRs.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from purely administrative payor calls into patient-facing navigation and clinical adherence.
- **M&A:** none visible
