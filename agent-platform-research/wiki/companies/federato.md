# Federato

**Thesis (one line):** An AI-native underwriting workbench for P&C insurers that automates submission triage, quoting, and portfolio steering to replace legacy underwriter desktops.

## Facts

- Founded: 2020
- HQ: San Francisco
- YC batch: none
- Backers: Goldman Sachs Alternatives (lead), Emergence Capital, Caffeinated Capital, StepStone Group, Pear VC
- Funding: >$180M total, $100M Series D (Nov 2025)
- Valuation: $751M (Nov 2025)
- Revenue/ARR: unknown (tripled between Nov 2024 and Nov 2025)
- ICP: Commercial P&C carriers, MGAs, and MGAAs
- Pricing model: unknown

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** unknown
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** proprietary connector library (sits on top of existing policy administration core)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** live sync with systems of record (unifies disparate internal and third-party data)
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** approval gates (executes on-strategy deals automatically under preset guardrails with underwriter audit sampling)
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC, audit trails, guardrails (codifies carrier constraints)
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** sales-led
9. **Moat thesis:** workflow lock-in, data gravity (AI learns from every decision across the policy lifecycle)
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** multi-step workflow (minutes) (submission triage to quote drafting)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end underwriting automation including submission scoring for appetite, real-time portfolio exposure surfacing, and drafting strategy-aligned quotes with reasoning.
- **Weaknesses:** High dependency on integration with legacy policy administration cores.
- **Trajectory:** Moving from a specialized tool to a full AI-native policy lifecycle system to replace "Old Core" legacy infrastructure.
- **M&A:** none visible
