## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Enables end-to-end testing of inbound/outbound voice agents via synthetic conversations, adversarial red-teaming, and live drift detection.
- **Weaknesses:** Focus is heavily weighted toward voice AI, which may limit general chat agent utility.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from simple testing into a full CI/CD pipeline for conversational AI with self-improving agent loops.
