# Idler

**Thesis (one line):** Idler provides RL environments and production-based data to train and evaluate AI models for expert-level coding capabilities.

## Facts

- Founded: 2025
- HQ: San Francisco, United States
- YC batch: S25
- Backers: Paradigm (lead)
- Funding: $9M Seed, 2026-08-19
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: $22.5M (2026-06-22, low confidence due to conflict with seed stage)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Enables models to perform expert-level engineering tasks by training them in realistic, scenario-based production environments.
- **Weaknesses:** High dependency on the continued demand for bespoke RL data from a small number of frontier labs.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from bespoke data work to offering off-the-shelf datasets across broader capabilities.
