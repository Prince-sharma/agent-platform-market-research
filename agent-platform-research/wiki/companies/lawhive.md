# Lawhive

**Thesis (one line):** An AI-powered legal operating system and law firm automating consumer legal services through AI-first intake and drafting.

## Facts

- Founded: unknown
- HQ: unknown
- YC batch: none

- Valuation: unknown

- ICP: Consumer legal clients and law firm partners
- Pricing model: Flat-fee per matter

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Natural language chat and dashboard for lawyers; AI-first intake layer for consumers.
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS (LegalOS).
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary LegalOS integrating intake, e-sign, and back-office accounting.
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Live sync with firm precedents, templates, and matter history.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Approval gates; every AI output requires attorney review before leaving the firm.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC, audit trails, regulatory-standard trust accounting, and AML/ID verification.
7. **Pricing model:** Flat-fee for consumers; platform-led back-office automation for partners.
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led expansion into US states; partner-led growth via "Co-Counsel" model.
9. **Moat thesis:** Workflow lock-in via LegalOS and data gravity from firm-specific precedents.
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary.
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow (minutes to days) covering intake, drafting, and case management.

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end automation of legal intake, conflict checks, document drafting using firm precedents, and back-office financial management.
- **Weaknesses:** High regulatory dependency across different US state jurisdictions.
- **Trajectory:** Scaling rapidly across 35 US states following Series B funding to disrupt fragmented consumer legal markets.
- **M&A:** none visible
