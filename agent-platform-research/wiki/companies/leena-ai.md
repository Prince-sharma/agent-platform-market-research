# Leena AI

**Thesis (one line):** An agentic AI platform providing vertical work agents for enterprise back-office functions like HR, IT, and finance to automate fragmented workflows.

## Facts

- Founded: 2018
- HQ: unknown
- YC batch: none
- Backers: Bessemer Venture Partners (led Series B), Greycroft, B Capital Group
- Funding: $40M total raised; $30M Series B (2021-09-28)
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown (reported $75M in contracts closed in a single day in August 2026)
- ICP: Large enterprises with fragmented back-office stacks (HR, IT, Finance, Procurement)
- Pricing model: Per user per year (headcount-based)

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Natural language (conversational AI platform)
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary connector library (200+ integrations including Workday, SAP, ServiceNow, Salesforce, Oracle)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Live sync with systems of record; sits on top of existing enterprise stack
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Fully autonomous for 70%+ of tickets; includes policy guardrails and role-based access
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC, full audit trails, and policy guardrails
7. **Pricing model:** Per seat (e.g., $150/user/year on 2-year subscription via Microsoft Marketplace)
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led and marketplace (Microsoft, SAP, AWS)
9. **Moat thesis:** Integration depth (200+ connectors) and workflow lock-in across fragmented back-office applications
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow (automates entire business processes end-to-end)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Agents act as "AI Colleagues" that resolve employee queries and automate workflows across HR, IT, Finance, and Procurement, reducing turnaround times (e.g., from 2 days to 6 hours).
- **Weaknesses:** High entry cost (1,000-seat floor) and lack of self-serve trials may alienate smaller enterprises.
- **Trajectory:** Moving from a specialized HR tool to a comprehensive back-office agentic platform, evidenced by the expansion into IT and Finance and massive 2026 contract growth.
- **M&A:** None visible.
