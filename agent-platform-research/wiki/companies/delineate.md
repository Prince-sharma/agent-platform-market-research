**Thesis (one line):** AI agents that accelerate clinical trial design by extracting and analyzing data from biopharma research papers and patents at scale.

1. **Build interface:** unknown

4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** built-in RAG (extracts data from research papers, patents, figures, and plots)
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** approval gates (mentions "rigorous QC processes")
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** unknown

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Agents perform systematic reviews by extracting structured data from unstructured scientific literature and visual plots to build specialized datasets for trial design.
- **Weaknesses:** High dependency on the accuracy of computer vision for complex scientific figures; narrow vertical focus.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from data extraction into deeper computational analysis workflows for drug development optimization.
