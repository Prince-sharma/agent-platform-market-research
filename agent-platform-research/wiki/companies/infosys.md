# Infosys

**Thesis (one line):** Global SI productizing AI agent delivery via Agentic Foundry and Topaz Fabric to scale enterprise automation through services-led deployment.

## Facts

- Founded: 1981
- HQ: Bangalore, India
- YC batch: none
- Backers: none; public company
- Funding: public company (NSE: INFY)
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Large enterprises across finance, healthcare, insurance, retail, telecom, and manufacturing
- Pricing model: unknown

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Hybrid; offers Agent-as-a-Service (framework-based) and pro-code solutions (graph-based plans)
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Multi-modal; supports cloud-first and on-premises AI factories (via NVIDIA Enterprise AI Factory design)
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Open architecture; supports Model Context Protocols (MCP) and Agent-to-Agent (A2A) protocols
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Built-in RAG and integration with enterprise data platforms via Topaz Fabric
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Supports both autonomous goal achievement and human-in-the-loop guardrails
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** Comprehensive AgentOps, evaluation frameworks, and responsible AI governance tooling
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** Services-led; leverages "forward-deployed engineers" to tailor and scale platform solutions
9. **Moat thesis:** Distribution via global SI footprint and deep vertical-specific pre-built agent libraries
10. **Open-source posture:** Technology-agnostic; utilizes open-source frameworks and LLMs to avoid vendor lock-in
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflows to long-horizon autonomous goal achievement

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end automation of business functions including invoice automation, audit processes, deep research for product insights, and retail loss prevention.
- **Weaknesses:** Heavy reliance on services-led implementation may slow velocity compared to pure SaaS agent platforms.
- **Trajectory:** Moving toward "service-as-software" by productizing reusable agent components and on-premises AI factories.
- **M&A:** none visible
