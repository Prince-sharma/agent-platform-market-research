# Paradox

**Thesis (one line):** A dominant vertical AI agent for high-volume hourly recruiting that automates candidate screening and scheduling via its assistant, Olivia.

## Facts

- Founded: 2016
- HQ: Scottsdale, AZ
- YC batch: none
- Backers: unknown
- Funding: $240.0M raised; Series C (2021-12-30)
- Valuation: $1.5B (2021/2022)
- Revenue/ARR: ~$116M ARR est. (2023)
- ICP: High-volume hourly/frontline hiring (retail, hospitality, healthcare, manufacturing) with 1,000+ hires/month.
- Pricing model: Annual contracts (Median: $142k for 1k-5k employees; $920k for 20k+ employees)

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Natural language (conversational AI via Olivia)
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary connector library (60+ integrations including Workday, SAP SuccessFactors, Oracle HCM)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Live sync with systems of record (ATS integration)
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Multi-step workflow with human-centric philosophy (AI handles routine duties, humans handle people-centric activity)
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC, audit trails, and compliance certs (SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, HIPAA, GDPR, CCPA, PCI DSS, FedRAMP)
7. **Pricing model:** Platform fee (Annual median based on company size)
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led (Enterprise focus)
9. **Moat thesis:** Workflow lock-in (deep ATS integrations) and distribution in high-volume hourly verticals.
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow (screening, scheduling, onboarding)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Olivia handles end-to-end candidate engagement: capturing and vetting candidates via chat, answering inquiries, and automating interview scheduling.
- **Weaknesses:** Less suited for low-volume professional hiring; variable conversational quality in non-English markets; narrow feature scope compared to full talent CRMs.
- **Trajectory:** Moving toward deeper conversational intelligence (launched Olivia Pro with GPT-4 depth in 2024) to maintain dominance in a thin long-tail HR market.
- **M&A:** Acquired/Merged on 2025-09-01 for $1.06B.
