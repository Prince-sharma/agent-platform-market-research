# Quivr

**Thesis (one line):** Open-source RAG "second brain" (28k GitHub stars) that pivoted to helpdesk auto-resolution, then wound down in April 2026 into The Vibe Company.

## Facts

- Founded: 2024 (per YC listing; open-source project dates to 2023)
- HQ: Paris, France
- YC batch: W24
- Backers: Y Combinator (W24); others unknown
- Funding: unknown beyond YC
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: about €10k MRR at peak, self-reported (April 2026); cash-flow positive with a small team
- ICP: Customer support teams using helpdesks (e.g., Zendesk)
- Pricing model: unknown

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** unknown
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Cloud or self-hosted (self-host positioned for data privacy)
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Worked inside helpdesk systems (e.g., Zendesk)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Open-source RAG framework connecting company data, docs, tools, APIs, and databases
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Auto-resolved tickets and drafted reply suggestions for agents; details unknown
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** unknown
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** Open-source community-led (28k GitHub stars) converting to a commercial support product
9. **Moat thesis:** Open-source community gravity as distribution
10. **Open-source posture:** OSS core; cloud or self-host
11. **Task horizon:** Single-ticket resolution (auto-resolve and reply drafting)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Drafted replies and auto-resolved support tickets inside helpdesks using RAG over company knowledge; did not progress to broader agent workflows.
- **Weaknesses:** Wound down April 2026: the team is now building The Vibe Company (AI workflow automation and delivery). Peak revenue only ~€10k MRR despite a 28k-star community; three positioning shifts in three years.
- **Trajectory:** Support product retired as of April 2026; founders Stan Girard, Brian Laleye, and Antoine Dewez now run The Vibe Company (cash-flow positive, French enterprise clients).
- **M&A:** none visible
