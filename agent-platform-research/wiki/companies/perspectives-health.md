## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Agents perform nightly audits of thousands of pages of clinical charts, identify documentation gaps that cause insurance denials, coordinate with clinicians via email to fix errors, and generate evidence for payer clawback disputes.
- **Weaknesses:** High dependency on the quality of unstructured clinical charts and clinician responsiveness to agent-initiated requests.
- **Trajectory:** Rapid early traction ($0 to $250k cARR in 4 weeks) suggests strong product-market fit for the "Reclaim" product in the behavioral health niche.
- **M&A:** none visible
