# Refactor

**Thesis (one line):** An enterprise AI orchestration platform providing secure, autonomous agents capable of navigating legacy software and complex workflows with robust governance.

- Founded: 2024
- HQ: unknown

- Pricing model: unknown

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** unknown
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** vendor multi-tenant SaaS (implied by deployment platform)
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers, Tabby MCP, and Aider
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Live sync with systems of record (payer portals, EHR/FHIR, ERA/835)
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Fully autonomous agents with red-teaming tools and governance models to detect/fix failures
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** Full audit trails, red-teaming for policy violations, and production failure logging
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led (targeting security/AI governance leaders at large enterprises)
9. **Moat thesis:** Evals and governance (turning prototypes into production-ready apps via safety testing)
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** Long-horizon (handles 45+ minute calls and complex multi-step decision trees)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Agents perform end-to-end RCM workflows, including navigating payer portals, executing denial management, and handling long-duration multilingual calls.
- **Weaknesses:** Early stage with limited public case studies beyond initial Fortune 50 pilots.
- **Trajectory:** Moving from a safety/evals focus (as Blast) toward a full-stack orchestration platform for legacy system automation.
- **M&A:** none visible
