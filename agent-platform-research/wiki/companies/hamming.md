# Hamming

**Thesis (one line):** An automated testing and monitoring platform that ensures reliability and compliance for AI voice agents through simulation and production analytics.

## Facts

- Founded: 2024
- HQ: San Francisco, CA
- YC batch: S24
- Backers: Mischief (lead), YCombinator, AI Grant, Pioneer, Coalition Operators, Coughdrop
- Funding: $4.3M total; $3.8M Seed on 2024-12-18
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Engineering and product teams building conversational AI phone agents in regulated industries (banks, healthtech).
- Pricing model: Custom pricing across three tiers (Startup, Agency, Enterprise).

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Dashboard for test generation, monitoring, and analytics; connects to frameworks like Vapi, Retell, and LiveKit.
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Multi-tenant SaaS.
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary connectors to voice agent frameworks.
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** AI generates test scenarios from the user's agent prompt.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Automated test call simulation and red-teaming with customizable alerts for human intervention.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA (BAA available), and trust and safety reports.
7. **Pricing model:** Custom pricing; includes platform fees, component costs (BYOK), and setup/integration fees.
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led (requires booking a sales call for pricing and signup).
9. **Moat thesis:** Evals and lifecycle coverage (testing, monitoring, and governance in one platform).
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary.
11. **Task horizon:** Single-turn to multi-step workflows (simulating phone calls).

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Enables teams to auto-generate hundreds of test scenarios, run 50K+ concurrent load tests, and monitor live production calls for hallucinations and latency.
- **Weaknesses:** Opaque pricing structure with no self-serve options or public rates, creating a "black box" evaluation process for users.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from simple QA into a full governance and compliance layer for regulated industries.
- **M&A:** none visible
