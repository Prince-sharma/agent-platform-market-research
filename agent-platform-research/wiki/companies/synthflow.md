# Synthflow

**Thesis (one line):** A no-code platform for deploying AI voice agents to automate inbound and outbound business telephony and customer interactions.

## Facts

- Founded: unknown
- HQ: unknown
- YC batch: none
- Backers: unknown
- Funding: $20M Series A (2025)
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Businesses requiring automated phone support, lead generation, and appointment scheduling
- Pricing model: Sales-led enterprise agreements and pay-as-you-go rates

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** No-code (Prompt Builder and Flow Designer) and natural language (Aurora assistant)
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary connector library (Salesforce, HubSpot, Stripe, WhatsApp)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Built-in knowledge base and live sync via APIs/webhooks
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Fixed workflow (Flow Designer) or open-ended (Prompt Builder) with call transfers
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** Workspace controls, procurement, and governance requirements
7. **Pricing model:** Platform fee plus usage (based on minutes, concurrency, and telephony setup)
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led for enterprise; self-serve for smaller accounts
9. **Moat thesis:** Workflow lock-in via Flow Designer and telephony integration depth
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow (minutes)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end automation of phone calls (inbound/outbound) and chat, including real-time booking, CRM updates, and information extraction.
- **Weaknesses:** High dependency on third-party telephony providers (e.g., Twilio) for certain channels.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from simple voice bots to complex, structured enterprise workflows using the BELL framework.
- **M&A:** none visible
