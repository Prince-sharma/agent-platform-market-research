# Cognition (Devin)

**Thesis (one line):** Cognition builds Devin, an autonomous AI software engineer that handles end-to-end engineering tasks from high-level requirements.

## Facts

- Founded: 2024
- HQ: unknown
- YC batch: none
- Backers: Andreessen Horowitz, Accel, Founders Fund, General Catalyst, Avenir (led Series E)
- Funding: Over $3 billion total (Series E $2B on 2026-09-08; previous round $1B in May 2026)
- Valuation: $48 billion (2026-09-08)
- Revenue/ARR: Nearly $900 million run-rate revenue (2026-09-08)
- ICP: Software engineering teams and enterprises
- Pricing model: Mixed; self-serve quotas and Enterprise Agent Compute Units (ACUs)

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Natural language requirements; integrates with Windsurf agentic IDE
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Multi-tenant SaaS (Devin Cloud); dedicated deployment option for Enterprise
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** unknown
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** unknown
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Fully autonomous; plans tasks, writes, tests, debugs, and pushes code
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** SAML/OIDC SSO, centralized enterprise admin controls, and Organization ACU limits
7. **Pricing model:** Self-serve tiers: Free ($0), Pro ($20/mo), Max ($200/mo), Teams ($80/mo platform + $40/mo per seat); Enterprise billed via ACUs
8. **GTM motion:** Hybrid PLG self-serve and sales-led Enterprise
9. **Moat thesis:** unknown
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary; provides access to leading open source models in Pro plan
11. **Task horizon:** Long-horizon; handles software engineering work end-to-end

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Devin operates from high-level requirements rather than line-by-line prompts. It autonomously plans tasks, writes and tests code, debugs, and pushes changes.
- **Weaknesses:** unknown
- **Trajectory:** Rapidly scaling revenue and valuation, expanding from a pure agent to an integrated agentic IDE ecosystem.
- **M&A:** Acquired Windsurf (agentic IDE) on 2025-07-14.
