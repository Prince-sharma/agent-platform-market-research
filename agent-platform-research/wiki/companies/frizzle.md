# Frizzle

**Thesis (one line):** AI-powered grading for handwritten math that converts paper-based student work into granular instructional data for K-12 teachers.

## Facts

- Founded: unknown
- HQ: unknown
- YC batch: S25
- Backers: unknown
- Funding: unknown
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: K-12 math teachers, schools, and districts
- Pricing model: Free for individual teachers; unknown for districts

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** unknown
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** unknown
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** proprietary model trained on 1.4 million pages of K-12 student work
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** approval gates (surfaces low-confidence pages for human check)
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** FERPA & COPPA compliant, SOC 2 Type II audited, AES-256 encryption
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** PLG self-serve (Free for individual teachers)
9. **Moat thesis:** data gravity (proprietary dataset of student handwriting and 147 named misconceptions)
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** single-turn (grading/parsing a stack of papers)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end processing of handwritten math: computer vision parses steps (not just answers), identifies specific misconceptions, and aggregates data into district-level dashboards.
- **Weaknesses:** Limited to a single vertical (math); traction remains early with no visible enterprise scale.
- **Trajectory:** Moving from a teacher-tool to a "live nervous system" for districts to track curriculum efficacy and equity gaps.
- **M&A:** none visible
