# Overlap

**Thesis (one line):** Multimodal AI agents that automate the end-to-end pipeline of converting long-form video into branded, social-ready short-form clips.

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** config workflow (node-based pipeline)
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** proprietary connector library (YouTube, Google Drive, Dropbox)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** live sync with systems of record
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** fully autonomous; supports workflows with or without humans in the loop
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** unknown
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** sales-led (Book a Demo)
9. **Moat thesis:** workflow lock-in
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** multi-step workflow (minutes)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Agents detect new uploads, identify engaging moments, reframe to vertical, add subtitles/B-roll/music, and schedule posts to social channels.
- **Weaknesses:** unknown
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from a clipping tool to a full "agentic" post-production platform for automated social feed management.
- **M&A:** none visible
