4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** live sync with systems of record (CRM, tool context)
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** enforces human escalation flows and approved tool execution
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** audit trails, compliance processes, and reporting on handoff trails
