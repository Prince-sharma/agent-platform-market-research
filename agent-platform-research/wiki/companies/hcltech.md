# HCLTech

**Thesis (one line):** Indian SI providing an enterprise-grade agentic AI platform to automate software engineering, IT operations, and business process workflows.

## Facts

- Founded: 1991
- HQ: Noida, India
- YC batch: none
- Backers: none; public company
- Funding: public company (NSE: HCLTECH)
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Large enterprises requiring AI-driven modernization of software and IT operations
- Pricing model: unknown

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Modular tooling including Agentic Studio, Prompt Builder, and RAG Builder
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Flexible; supports on-premises, hybrid cloud, private cloud, public cloud, and edge
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary connectors for IDEs (VS Code, Eclipse), Jira, and CMS; strategic collaboration with Pega Blueprint
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Built-in RAG pipelines connecting enterprise data sources, APIs, and repositories
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Autonomous AI agents with embedded Responsible AI evaluators for governance and auditability
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC, governance guardrails, observability, and Responsible AI evaluators
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** Services-led; integrated with modernization and transformation engagements
9. **Moat thesis:** Distribution via global SI footprint and deep integration into the software engineering lifecycle
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary; model-agnostic support for OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, Llama, and others
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflows across the full software engineering and IT operations lifecycle

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Agents automate end-to-end software/data engineering, IT operations, and business processes, from requirements gathering to maintenance.
- **Weaknesses:** Heavy reliance on services-led delivery may slow adoption compared to pure SaaS agent platforms.
- **Trajectory:** Moving toward a "single intelligence fabric" (AI Force 2.0) that unifies engineering, operations, and applications.
- **M&A:** none visible
