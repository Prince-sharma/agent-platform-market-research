# Salient

**Thesis (one line):** Vertical AI platform providing compliant voice and omnichannel agents for consumer loan servicing, collections, and automated auditing.

- HQ: San Francisco
- YC batch: W23
- Backers: Andreessen Horowitz, Matrix Partners, Michael Ovitz, Y Combinator
- Funding: $75M total; $60M Series A1 (July 2025)
- Valuation: $500M (December 2025)
- Revenue/ARR: $25M ARR (December 2025, high confidence)
- ICP: Banks, credit unions, auto lenders, and fintechs
- Pricing model: unknown

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** unknown
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** proprietary connector library (integrates with existing lending software/LMS)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** live sync with systems of record (LMS-level controls)
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** approval gates (native FDCPA, TCPA, and CFPB compliance built in)
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC, audit trails (Marshall automated audit engine), compliance certs
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** sales-led (partners with technology-first lenders)
9. **Moat thesis:** workflow lock-in, regulatory compliance, integrations
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** multi-step workflow (minutes)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end automation of collections (negotiating payments, promises to pay), customer service, total-loss insurance claims, and real-time 100% audit coverage of interactions.
- **Weaknesses:** Highly concentrated in the consumer lending vertical; potential sensitivity to changes in federal lending regulations.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from voice-first collections into a comprehensive AI-native servicing infrastructure including automated auditing and claims management.
- **M&A:** none visible
