# Penciled

**Thesis (one line):** AI front office assistant automating scheduling, reminders, and waitlist management for physical therapy practices.

- YC batch: W24

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** natural language (phone-based AI assistants)
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** proprietary connector library (integrates with EHRs like WebPT, fax machines, and phone systems)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** live sync with systems of record (EHRs)
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** fully autonomous for repetitive tasks (scheduling, reminders, cancellations)
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** unknown
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** sales-led (offering free integrations for early adopters)
9. **Moat thesis:** integrations (deep EHR and legacy system connectivity)
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** multi-step workflow (minutes)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Handles end-to-end front office phone tasks: scheduling plans of care, sending reminders, processing cancellations, and managing automated waitlists.
- **Weaknesses:** Highly verticalized focus on physical therapy may limit total addressable market unless expanded to other specialties.
- **Trajectory:** Moving toward deeper EHR integration to replace manual administrative phone labor in clinics.
- **M&A:** none visible
