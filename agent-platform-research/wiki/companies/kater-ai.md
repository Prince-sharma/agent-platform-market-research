# kater.ai

**Thesis (one line):** An AI-driven decision engineering platform that transforms single business questions into structured, repeatable data analysis playbooks.

## Facts

- Founded: 2023
- HQ: San Francisco
- YC batch: W24
- Backers: Y Combinator
- Funding: unknown
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Data professionals and business stakeholders (executives)
- Pricing model: Annual contracts based on data size and company complexity

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Natural language and structured decision trees (Playbooks)
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary connectors for Snowflake, BigQuery, Databricks, Redshift, and MS-SQL
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Shared semantic layer, RAG, and AgentMesh Framework for data mapping
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Human-in-the-loop (AI assists data teams in writing SQL for Playbooks)
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** SOC 2, ISO 27001, PII labeling, and unified governance
7. **Pricing model:** Platform fee (annual contracts)
8. **GTM motion:** PLG self-serve (free trials) and sales-led (annual contracts)
9. **Moat thesis:** Workflow lock-in via proprietary "Playbooks" and captured tribal knowledge in the semantic layer
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary; supports self-hosted or managed LLMs
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow (minutes)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Butler AI indexes data maps, generates hypotheses, writes SQL/Python to validate them, and delivers actionable insights via decision trees.
- **Weaknesses:** Reliance on data teams to initially build and verify the "Playbook" logic to ensure trust.
- **Trajectory:** Moving from reactive dashboards to proactive "decision engineering" by tracking the impact of data-driven decisions.
- **M&A:** none visible
