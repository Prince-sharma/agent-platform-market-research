**Thesis (one line):** AI agent platform automating mechanical design by connecting CAD, PLM, and supplier tools to streamline hardware engineering workflows.

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Natural language (chat) and parametric sliders
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS (private Linux sandbox per user)
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** MCP (Model Context Protocol) and proprietary connectors (Onshape, Fusion, SolidWorks, etc.)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Live sync with systems of record (CAD, PLM, supplier tools)
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Approval gates (checkpoints for plans, drafts, and file changes)
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** Trust & Compliance (security and subprocessors mentioned)
7. **Pricing model:** Per seat (subscription tiers)
8. **GTM motion:** PLG self-serve (web app)
9. **Moat thesis:** Integrations and workflow lock-in across CAD/PLM/Supplier ecosystem
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary (with CADAM open-source text-to-CAD generator)
11. **Task horizon:** Long-horizon (background tasks with persistent sandboxes)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Performs end-to-end mechanical design tasks including text-to-CAD generation, parametric edits, BOM management, sourcing, and drafting RFQs.
- **Weaknesses:** unknown
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from a text-to-CAD editor into a comprehensive "Cursor for CAD" co-pilot integrated into professional workflows.
- **M&A:** none visible
