# Ivo

**Thesis (one line):** AI agents for in-house legal teams to automate contract review, redlining, and verified legal research.

## Facts

- Founded: unknown
- HQ: unknown
- YC batch: none
- Backers: Blackbird
- Funding: $55M+ (Series B, 2026-01-20)
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: In-house legal teams and General Counsel
- Pricing model: Reported $6,000 per user per year (flat annual fee)

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Natural language prompts via AI Assistant; integrated directly into Microsoft Word.
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS; SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified.
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary connectors for cloud storage, CRMs, e-signature vendors, and file systems.
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Built-in RAG using customer playbooks, historical documents, and 100+ trusted legal databases (EDGAR, U.S. Code).
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Approval gates; agents perform redlining and research for human lawyer review and editing.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC, audit trails, SOC 2/ISO 27001; does not train on customer data.
7. **Pricing model:** Per seat ($6,000/user/year); all-inclusive of onboarding and playbook creation.
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led; demo required for custom quotes.
9. **Moat thesis:** Workflow lock-in via Word integration and proprietary legal playbooks.
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary.
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow (minutes) for review; multi-agent workflows for complex legal research.

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end contract redlining based on playbooks, cross-document comparison, and multi-agent legal research with traceable citations.
- **Weaknesses:** High per-seat cost and lack of self-serve entry may limit adoption for smaller firms.
- **Trajectory:** Scaling from a contract review tool to a comprehensive "agentic legal partner" for in-house counsel.
- **M&A:** None visible.
