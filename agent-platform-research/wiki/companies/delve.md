# Delve

**Thesis (one line):** AI agent platform that automates enterprise compliance monitoring and evidence collection to accelerate regulatory certifications for startups and SaaS companies.

## Facts

- Founded: 2024
- HQ: San Francisco
- YC batch: W24
- Backers: Insight Partners (lead), Fortune 500 CISOs
- Funding: $35.3M total ($32M Series A, July 2025; $3.3M Seed, January 2025)
- Valuation: $300M (July 2025)
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Startups and growing SaaS teams seeking first-time certifications (SOC 2, HIPAA, GDPR, ISO 27001)
- Pricing model: Custom yearly bundle (platform, auditor, pentest)
