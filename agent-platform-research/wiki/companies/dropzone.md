# Dropzone

**Thesis (one line):** Dropzone provides autonomous AI SOC analysts that automate Tier 1 alert triage and investigation to reduce analyst burnout and MTTR.

## Facts

- Founded: unknown
- HQ: Seattle, WA
- YC batch: none
- Backers: Theory Ventures (lead), Madrona, Decibel Ventures, Pioneer Square Labs, IQT
- Funding: $57M+ total; $37M Series B (Jul 2025)
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: SOC teams, MSSPs, and enterprises overwhelmed by alert volume
- Pricing model: Sales-quoted tiers (Base, Enterprise, MSSP) as of 2026; historically per AI analyst

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Chatbot and web interface for accessing reports and fine-tuning AI Analyst behavior.
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** unknown
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Integrations with 80+ to 90+ security tools.
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Pre-trained AI agents that replicate investigative processes and techniques of expert analysts.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Fully autonomous Tier 1 alert triage and investigation; provides detailed write-ups for human review.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** unknown
7. **Pricing model:** Historically $36,000/year per AI analyst (up to 4,000 investigations); moved to sales-quoted Base, Enterprise, and MSSP tiers in 2026.
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led (moved to sales-quoted tiers in 2026).
9. **Moat thesis:** Pre-trained investigative techniques and deep integrations across security toolsets.
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow (investigates alerts and provides detailed reports).

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** The agents autonomously handle Tier 1 alert triage and investigation across phishing, endpoint, network, cloud, and identity alerts. They replicate expert analyst techniques to provide full-depth investigations and detailed reports 24/7.
- **Weaknesses:** Current value is heavily weighted toward triage; promised Threat Hunter and Intel Analyst agents were not available until Fall 2026.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from a triage-focused tool to a broader autonomous SOC platform including threat hunting and intelligence agents.
- **M&A:** none visible
