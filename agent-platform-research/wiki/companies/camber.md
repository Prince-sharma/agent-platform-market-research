# Camber

**Thesis (one line):** AI-driven revenue cycle management (RCM) platform automating claims, eligibility, and reimbursement for specialty healthcare providers.

## Facts

- Founded: 2021
- HQ: New York City, NY
- YC batch: Winter 2021
- Backers: a16z (lead), Craft Ventures, YCombinator, Manresa
- Funding: $50.1M total; $30M Series B (Feb 2025)
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Specialty care provider groups (including behavioral health)
- Pricing model: unknown

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** unknown
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** proprietary connector library (integrations with EHRs, clearinghouses, and LLM services)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** built-in RAG; trained on $2.5B+ in real claims data
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** approval gates; AI-driven investigation with human-in-the-loop for final judgment calls
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** unknown
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** sales-led (demo-based)
9. **Moat thesis:** data gravity (collective intelligence from network-wide claims data) and workflow lock-in
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** multi-step workflow (minutes to days for claim lifecycle)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end RCM automation including eligibility verification, claims creation, pre-submission scrubbing, and denial management using a rules engine that evolves based on payer behavior.
- **Weaknesses:** High dependency on the accuracy of third-party EHR and clearinghouse integrations.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from behavioral health into additional healthcare treatment verticals.
- **M&A:** none visible
