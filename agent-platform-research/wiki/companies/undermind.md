# Undermind

**Thesis (one line):** An AI agent for scientific research that uses adaptive deep search to find critical literature missed by traditional keyword search engines.

## Facts

- Founded: 2023
- HQ: San Francisco
- YC batch: S24
- Backers: Y Combinator
- Funding: unknown
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Research scientists, doctors, and pharmaceutical R&D teams
- Pricing model: Tiered subscription (Free, Pro, Team, Enterprise)

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** natural language
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** MCP
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** built-in RAG (accesses ArXiv and other scientific databases)
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** fully autonomous search agent with human-led iteration on reports
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC, admin dashboard, custom SLA/security review, no-training guarantee
7. **Pricing model:** per seat
8. **GTM motion:** PLG self-serve and sales-led (Enterprise)
9. **Moat thesis:** evals (10-50x better recall than keyword search), proprietary adaptive search algorithm
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** multi-step workflow (minutes)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Performs iterative "Deep Search" by reading papers, following citation trails, and adapting queries to find all relevant literature.
- **Weaknesses:** Higher latency than keyword search (results take several minutes); limited to scientific literature databases.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from a standalone search tool to infrastructure for other AI research agents and enterprise R&D stacks.
- **M&A:** none visible
