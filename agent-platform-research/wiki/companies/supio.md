# Supio

**Thesis (one line):** Agentic AI platform for plaintiff personal injury and mass tort firms to automate medical chronologies and case analysis.

## Facts

- Founded: unknown
- HQ: Seattle or San Francisco
- YC batch: none
- Backers: Mayfield
- Funding: $60M (Series B, 2025-04-30)
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Plaintiff personal injury and mass tort law firms
- Pricing model: Custom; estimated $150-$400 per user/month or per-case pricing

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Natural language (AI Chat Assistant) and structured output (chronologies/demands); or unknown
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS; SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, PHIPA, and GDPR compliant
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary connectors and Supio API for case management systems
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Live sync with systems of record; data does not train foundation models
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Agentic workflows for drafting and analysis with attorney review for final strategy
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC, SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, PHIPA, GDPR compliance; data centers in US, UK, Canada
7. **Pricing model:** Custom quotes; estimated tiered per-user ($150-$400/mo) or per-case analyzed
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led (demo-based pricing)
9. **Moat thesis:** Vertical-specific data gravity (medical records), workflow lock-in, and partnerships (Thomson Reuters)
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow (minutes to days for chronologies and demand letters)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end automation of medical record analysis, creation of case chronologies, and drafting of settlement demand letters.
- **Weaknesses:** High dependency on the accuracy of unstructured medical record OCR and interpretation.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from simple document analysis to "firm-level" agents that manage institutional knowledge and practice visibility.
- **M&A:** none visible
