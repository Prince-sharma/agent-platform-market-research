# Edexia

**Thesis (one line):** An AI teacher assistant for grading essays across global curricula, reducing teacher workload through high-accuracy, rubric-aligned automated feedback.

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** unknown
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** unknown
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** built-in RAG (pre-loaded rubrics, study designs, and text-specific knowledge bases)
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** approval gates (teacher review mode; ability to edit/delete AI comments before release)
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC, compliance certs (ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II), data de-identification
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** PLG self-serve (offers free access with no account needed)
9. **Moat thesis:** evals (validated accuracy against teacher judgment), distribution (trusted by 50+ schools)
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** multi-step workflow (grading, feedback generation, and moderation)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end essay grading including handwriting transcription, rubric-aligned scoring, AI-likelihood detection, and cross-submission progress reporting.
- **Weaknesses:** Traction remains early/unverified at scale; limited to specific educational curricula.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from IB English into other regional Australian and international curricula.
- **M&A:** none visible
