# Booko

**Thesis (one line):** AI-driven dynamic pricing engine that predicts empty time slots and applies discounts to maximize revenue for service-based businesses.

## Facts

- Founded: 2025
- HQ: San Francisco
- YC batch: W26
- Backers: Y Combinator
- Funding: unknown
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: High fixed-cost businesses selling time slots (fitness studios, med-spas, consultants, event venues)
- Pricing model: unknown

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** config workflow
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** proprietary connector library
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** live sync with systems of record
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** fully autonomous; guardrails
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** SOC 2 Compliant, HIPAA Compliant, Enterprise Security
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** sales-led
9. **Moat thesis:** data gravity
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** persistent (always-on)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Predicts slot fill rates using historical data and automatically surfaces targeted incentives/discounts through existing booking flows.
- **Weaknesses:** Dependency on third-party booking system APIs (e.g., MindBody, Mariana Tek).
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from niche service providers (fitness/med-spas) to "dynamically pricing the whole economy."
- **M&A:** none visible
