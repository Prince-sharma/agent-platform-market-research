## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Config workflow with a data marketplace and "Claygent" AI research agents.
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS.
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary connector library (200+ providers), API, CLI, and MCP for rep tools.
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Live sync with systems of record (CRM enrichment) and built-in data marketplace.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Approval gates via workflow orchestration; Claygents perform autonomous research.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** unknown
7. **Pricing model:** Platform fee plus usage (credits for actions and data).
8. **GTM motion:** PLG self-serve (14-day trial) and sales-led for enterprise.
9. **Moat thesis:** Data gravity (200+ providers), workflow lock-in, and creation of the "GTM engineer" persona.
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary.
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow (minutes) to persistent (always-on) CRM enrichment.

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Claygents perform end-to-end research on target companies and people to automate personalized outbound and TAM sourcing.
- **Weaknesses:** High dependency on third-party data provider stability and credit-cost volatility.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from a data tool to a full GTM orchestration platform, integrating deeper into rep workflows via MCP.
- **M&A:** none visible
