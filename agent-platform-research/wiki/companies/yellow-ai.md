# Yellow.ai

**Thesis (one line):** Enterprise agentic AI platform automating customer and employee experiences across voice, chat, and email to replace traditional BPO functions.

## Facts

- Founded: unknown
- HQ: unknown
- YC batch: none

- Funding: ~$550M SPAC merger announced Aug 2026
- Valuation: ~$550M (Aug 2026)
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Global brands in BFSI, Healthcare, Utilities, and Retail seeking CX/EX automation
- Pricing model: Tiered (Free, Enterprise) with per-resolution fees for lower tiers

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Natural language prompts and AI Agent Builder 2.0
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary connector library with 150+ pre-built enterprise integrations
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Built-in Agentic RAG via Conversational Knowledge Base
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Fully autonomous agents with Agent Assist for human-AI collaboration and guardrails
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC, SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, ISO 27701, HIPAA, and PCI-DSS V 4.0.1
7. **Pricing model:** Platform fee plus usage (per resolution for free tier)
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led for enterprise; PLG self-serve for free tier
9. **Moat thesis:** Distribution via BPO acquisition strategy and multi-LLM flexibility to avoid vendor lock-in
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow (minutes) for autonomous resolution of customer and employee queries

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end autonomous resolution of CX/EX tasks across voice, chat, and email using 15+ LLMs and RAG.
- **Weaknesses:** Heavy reliance on the success of the SPAC-funded BPO acquisition strategy to maintain growth.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from a software provider to a service-replacement entity via a $550M public listing to acquire BPOs.
- **M&A:** Announced merger with Bluerock Acquisition Corp (Nasdaq: BLRK) in August 2026.
