1. **Build interface:** unknown

5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Internal-facing automation (QA review, coaching triggers, training simulations) rather than autonomous customer resolution
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** unknown

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** AI QA reviews every support conversation (human and AI agents), surfaces risk and skill gaps, and triggers personalized coaching and training simulations; founders Mark Hughes (CEO) and Patrick Finlay (CTO).
- **Weaknesses:** QA/coaching is feature-adjacent to helpdesk and incumbent QA vendors; value depends on integration depth with Intercom, Zendesk, and Gong.
- **Trajectory:** Seed (Jun 2025) to $25M Series A led by Hedosophia (Apr 2026); positioning broadened from training-first to AI-native QA platform.
- **M&A:** none visible
