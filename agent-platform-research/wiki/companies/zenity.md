# Zenity

**Thesis (one line):** An end-to-end security and governance platform providing discovery, posture management, and runtime enforcement for enterprise AI agents.

## Facts

- Founded: 2020
- HQ: Tel Aviv
- YC batch: none
- Backers: Norwest (lead), SoftBank Vision Fund 2, Qumra Capital, Hitachi Ventures, LG Technology Ventures, DTCP, Vertex Ventures, Third Point Ventures, Intel Capital
- Funding: $180M total; $125M Series C (Aug 03, 2026)
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: Revenues tripled annually over the past two years (as of Aug 2026)
- ICP: Fortune 500 enterprises, security and compliance teams, organizations using low-code/no-code platforms
- Pricing model: Custom pricing

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Dashboards for observability and governance; runtime enforcement engine for steering actions.
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Multi-tenant SaaS (available via AWS and Azure Marketplaces).
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary connectors for Copilot Studio, Agentforce, Bedrock, Azure AI Foundry, Vertex AI, and ChatGPT Enterprise.
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Live sync with systems of record to monitor agent behavior, permissions, and dependencies.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Enforces real-time guardrails to block or shut down agents based on intent-aware detection.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** AI Security Posture Management (AISPM), agent identity management (integrates with Okta and Microsoft Entra), and audit trails.
7. **Pricing model:** Custom enterprise pricing.
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led; available via cloud marketplaces.
9. **Moat thesis:** Deep integration across diverse agent platforms and "intent-aware" runtime enforcement that goes beyond model-layer security.
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary.
11. **Task horizon:** Persistent (always-on monitoring and runtime enforcement).

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Enables enterprises to deploy autonomous agents by providing a safety layer that discovers agents, manages their posture, and blocks malicious runtime actions.
- **Weaknesses:** Resource-intensive implementation not suitable for small teams; high commitment required for deployment.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding globally to secure the "era of 1 billion agents" with a focus on context-responsive policies and dynamic permissions.
- **M&A:** none visible
