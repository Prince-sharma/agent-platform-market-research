# Roark

**Thesis (one line):** An observability and testing platform that enables voice AI developers to simulate scenarios and score production calls to improve agent reliability.

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Enables agents to be tested against hundreds of simulated scenarios and monitored via 64+ audio-native metrics.
- **Weaknesses:** Focuses exclusively on the voice modality, limiting its utility for text-only or multimodal agents.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from simple monitoring to a full "self-improvement loop" combining production scoring with simulation.
