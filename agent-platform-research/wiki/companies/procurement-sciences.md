# Procurement Sciences

**Thesis (one line):** An AI-native operating system for government contractors to automate the find-win-deliver lifecycle of federal procurement.

- Founded: unknown

- Pricing model: unknown

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Natural language (AI Chat) and structured platform modules (Pipeline, Datahub, Bid Search)
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary platform integrating bid search, CRM-like pipeline, and document hubs
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Built-in RAG using 100M+ indexed data points of winning proposals and company-specific Datahub
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Approval gates; AI handles execution (drafting/review) while humans focus on strategy and positioning
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** Built for security; includes compliance matrices and automated compliance reviews
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led (demo-based) with a "Free AI Win Strategy" lead magnet
9. **Moat thesis:** Data gravity (100M+ winning proposal data points) and deep vertical workflow lock-in for GovCon
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow (minutes to days) covering bid search, bid/no-bid decisioning, and proposal drafting

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end automation of the GovCon lifecycle: identifying opportunities via smart matching, calculating PWIN, drafting compliant proposals, and performing automated compliance reviews.
- **Weaknesses:** High dependency on the specific regulatory environment of government contracting, limiting horizontal expansion.
- **Trajectory:** Moving toward a full "operating system" for GovCon, expanding from simple drafting to predictive bid/no-bid analytics.
- **M&A:** none visible
