# Beacon Health

**Thesis (one line):** AI employees that automate end-to-end EHR workflows for primary care practices to enable scalable value-based care.

- Founded: unknown
- HQ: San Francisco
- YC batch: W26

- Pricing model: unknown

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** natural language (implied) / EHR-native (no new portals)
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** unknown
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** proprietary (agents learn to use EHR like a human)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** live sync with systems of record (EHR)
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** fully autonomous (end-to-end ownership of workflows)
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** unknown
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** sales-led (targeting risk-based organizations)
9. **Moat thesis:** workflow lock-in (EHR automation) and vertical specialization (value-based care)
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** multi-step workflow (minutes to hours)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Agents execute end-to-end administrative tasks directly within the EHR, including closing quality gaps, executing patient outreach, automating lab orders, and capturing risk adjustment codes.
- **Weaknesses:** High dependency on the ability to navigate fragmented and legacy EHR interfaces without native APIs.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from initial value-based care autopilot tools to broader "AI employee" roles for primary care operations.
- **M&A:** none visible
