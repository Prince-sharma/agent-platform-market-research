# Bland

**Thesis (one line):** An enterprise voice-agent platform using in-house models to handle complex, non-linear phone conversations at scale.

## Facts

- Founded: unknown
- HQ: unknown
- YC batch: YC S23

- Funding: $100M+ total; Series C, 2026-06-16
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Enterprises in healthcare, financial services, and high-stakes industries requiring scalable phone automation.
- Pricing model: Per-minute usage plus optional monthly platform fee.

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** unknown
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Multi-tenant SaaS with On-prem / VPC available for Enterprise.
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary connectors including SMS, iMessage, and appointment scheduling nodes.
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Built-in knowledge bases.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Fully autonomous with guardrails (Protected Calls) and live/warm transfers.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** HIPAA, SOC 2, BAA, SSO, JWT signatures, and data residency.
7. **Pricing model:** Platform fee plus per-minute usage (no token or model pass-through charges).
8. **GTM motion:** PLG self-serve (Start/Build/Scale tiers) and sales-led Enterprise.
9. **Moat thesis:** In-house voice models purpose-built for latency and interruptions.
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary; self-host option available for Enterprise.
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow (calls ranging from 30 to 45 minutes).

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end phone automation capable of handling meandering, non-linear conversations, emotional intelligence, and real-time data extraction.
- **Weaknesses:** unknown
- **Trajectory:** Moving toward high-stakes, complex conversations by replacing general-purpose models with proprietary voice-native architecture.
- **M&A:** none visible
