# NOSO LABS

**Thesis (one line):** AI agents for field technicians that automate diagnosis, sales follow-ups, and paperwork to increase revenue without increasing headcount.

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** natural language (text/phone)
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** proprietary connector library (syncs with CRM and ERP)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** live sync with systems of record
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** approval gates (95% automated, 5% escalated to back office)
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC, audit trails, SOC 2 Type I, SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, CCPA
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** unknown
9. **Moat thesis:** workflow lock-in (integrations with CRM/ERP and technician habits)
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** long-horizon (handles follow-ups and conversion over time)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Agents handle the end-to-end administrative lifecycle of a field job: receiving technician updates via text, syncing data to CRM/ERP, following up on unsold estimates to convert them to revenue, and escalating complex requests (financing/approvals) to humans.
- **Weaknesses:** No visible market traction reported in census data.
- **Trajectory:** Positioning as a vertical L3 agent in the field services space, fitting a YC pattern of vertical voice/text agents.
- **M&A:** none visible
