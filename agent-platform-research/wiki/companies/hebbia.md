# Hebbia

**Thesis (one line):** Agentic document analysis platform enabling finance and legal professionals to perform structured, scalable due diligence across massive private datasets.

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Matrix interface; structured workflows that carry analysis to final deliverables (memos, decks, models).
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** vendor multi-tenant SaaS; available via AWS Marketplace.
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Integrates with internal systems, external data feeds, and private document stores.
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Indexes firm-specific deal history, proprietary intelligence, and private documents for RAG-based analysis.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Approval gates; every workflow includes checkpoints for professional sign-off before subsequent steps run.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** Full traceability; users can verify the source of every number and conclusion.
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** sales-led; includes professional services to index firm-specific processes and history.
9. **Moat thesis:** Workflow lock-in via proprietary deal history indexing and differentiated professional processes.
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** multi-step workflow (minutes to hours) covering the entire diligence process to final deliverable.

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Agents perform multi-document extraction, benchmarking, and synthesis, producing final investment memos, financial models, and pitch decks.
- **Weaknesses:** High dependency on professional oversight to ensure "judgment" and quality bars are met.
- **Trajectory:** Moving from simple document Q&A to end-to-end "work-agent" automation for the entire diligence lifecycle.
- **M&A:** none visible
