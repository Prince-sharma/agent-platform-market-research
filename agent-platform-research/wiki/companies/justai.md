# JustAI

**Thesis (one line):** An agentic marketing platform using reinforcement learning to automate hyper-personalized lifecycle messaging and decision-making for growth teams.

## Facts

- Founded: 2023 or 2024 (approx. 2.5 years prior to June 2026)
- HQ: unknown
- YC batch: Yes
- Backers: Base10 (lead), Peak XV, Y-Combinator
- Funding: $17.5M total; Series A June 2026
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Growth and lifecycle marketers at companies like Grammarly, BeReal, Notion
- Pricing model: unknown

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Natural language/builder tools designed to make marketers "builders" without needing engineers.
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS.
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** unknown
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Live sync with user attributes and behavior data to feed decisioning engines.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Fully autonomous decisioning via contextual bandits; marketers author the variants the agents select from.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** unknown
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led/Product-led (evidenced by "Book a demo" and power customer feedback loops).
9. **Moat thesis:** Reinforcement learning (contextual bandits) and data gravity from performance loops.
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary.
11. **Task horizon:** Persistent (always-on agents for lifecycle marketing).

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Agents automate the selection of messaging variants (tone, theme, framing) using Disjoint Linear Thompson Sampling to optimize for specific user attributes.
- **Weaknesses:** Requires high traffic volumes and rich user attribute data to avoid falling back to standard non-contextual bandits.
- **Trajectory:** Moving from a simple optimization layer to "tearing apart the entire marketing stack" to build a full agentic infrastructure.
- **M&A:** none visible
