# Luminance

**Thesis (one line):** Enterprise legal AI platform providing autonomous contract negotiation, review, and drafting for Global 100 law firms and Fortune 500 companies.

## Facts

- Founded: unknown
- HQ: unknown
- YC batch: none
- Backers: Point72 Private Investments (lead), Mike Lynch
- Funding: $125M+ total; $75M Series C (Feb 2025)
- Valuation: $100M (2019)
- Revenue/ARR: $30M ARR (late 2024, high confidence)
- ICP: Fortune 500 legal departments and Global 100 law firms
- Pricing model: Custom enterprise subscription with document-volume metering; some plans $10–$100/user/month

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Natural language and Microsoft Word integration; modular suite (Draft, Negotiate, Analyze, Comply, Investigate, Collaborate).
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS; available via AWS Marketplace.
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary connector library; deep Microsoft Word integration for redlining.
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Built-in RAG; trained on 150M legal documents; "Institutional Memory" for cross-contract intelligence.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Fully autonomous agent-to-agent negotiation by default; human-in-the-loop by choice with transparent reasoning.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC; audit trails; legal standards enforcement; governance oversight for non-legal users.
7. **Pricing model:** Platform fee plus usage (document-volume metering); typical average deal value $42,857 annually.
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led enterprise; AWS Marketplace private offers.
9. **Moat thesis:** Data gravity (150M documents), proprietary "Legal-Grade AI" Mixture of Experts architecture, and workflow lock-in.
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary.
11. **Task horizon:** Long-horizon (autonomous end-to-end contract negotiation and lifecycle management).

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Agents perform autonomous AI-to-AI negotiation, redlining terms based on legal standards, and cross-portfolio risk analysis.
- **Weaknesses:** High entry cost and complexity of custom enterprise negotiations may limit mid-market adoption.
- **Trajectory:** Moving toward "Enterprise-Wide" AI, enabling non-legal staff to negotiate contracts while Legal maintains governance.
- **M&A:** None visible.
