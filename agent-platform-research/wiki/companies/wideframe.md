# Wideframe

**Thesis (one line):** AI assistant editor that automates footage preparation and sequencing to accelerate professional video production workflows.

## Facts

- Founded: 2025
- HQ: San Francisco
- YC batch: W26
- Backers: Y Combinator
- Funding: unknown
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Professional video editors, producers, and content teams
- Pricing model: $100/mo subscription

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** natural language
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** native desktop app (Mac/Apple Silicon)
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** proprietary connector library; MCP; native Adobe Premiere Pro (.prproj) integration
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** live sync with systems of record (local filesystem and cloud storage)
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** approval gates (outputs rough cuts for human refinement in NLE)
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** unknown
7. **Pricing model:** per seat (subscription)
8. **GTM motion:** PLG self-serve (7-day free trial)
9. **Moat thesis:** workflow lock-in (Premiere Pro roundtrip), data gravity (indexing local/cloud libraries)
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** multi-step workflow (minutes)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Indexes footage by meaning, performs agentic search, labels clips, and assembles native Premiere Pro sequences from natural language prompts.
- **Weaknesses:** Platform lock-in to macOS/Apple Silicon; dependency on Adobe Premiere Pro for final output.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from a "Claude Code for Video" prep tool to a default collaborative workspace for humans and agents.
- **M&A:** none visible
