# Bunkerhill Health

**Thesis (one line):** An agentic AI platform enabling health systems to rapidly build and deploy custom clinical and operational agents via its Carebricks layer.

- Founded: unknown
- HQ: San Francisco
- YC batch: none

- Valuation: unknown

- Pricing model: unknown

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Natural language (teams describe problems, logic, and desired actions to create agents)
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS (implied by "platform" and "deployment")
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Deep healthcare integrations; operates directly within live clinical and administrative systems
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Live sync with systems of record (reasons across encounters, conditions, labs, and imaging reports)
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Approval gates (sites may optionally insert manual approval steps before reordering or messaging)
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** unknown
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led (enterprise-scale deployments at health systems)
9. **Moat thesis:** Workflow lock-in and data gravity via deep integration into hospital operations and clinician-led custom agent creation
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow (minutes to days; e.g., identifying a gap, routing a patient, and generating tasks)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Agents perform end-to-end operational and clinical tasks: scanning imaging reports for incidental findings, reordering referral queues by clinical urgency, automating prior authorizations, and assembling data for tumor boards.
- **Weaknesses:** Heavy reliance on the quality of existing health system data (labs, notes, imaging) to trigger agent actions.
- **Trajectory:** Moving toward becoming the "connective tissue" or operating system for clinician-led AI innovation across the entire hospital enterprise.
- **M&A:** none visible
