# Giga AI

**Thesis (one line):** Voice-first, "emotionally intelligent" AI agents for enterprise customer service, pivoted from on-prem LLMs; $61M Series A targets an enterprise operations platform.

## Facts

- Founded: 2023 as GigaML (YC directory lists 2024), by Varun Vummadi (CEO) and Esha Manideep (CTO; IIT Kharagpur CS 2023)
- HQ: San Francisco
- YC batch: S23
- Backers: Redpoint Ventures (Series A lead), Nexus Venture Partners (seed lead), Y Combinator
- Funding: ~$64.6M total ($3.6M seed led by Nexus, Oct 2023, as GigaML; $61M Series A led by Redpoint, 2025-11-05)
- Valuation: unknown (not disclosed post-Series A)
- Revenue/ARR: unknown; DoorDash live-delivery workflows at over 90% resolve rate (Sep-Oct 2025 data)
- ICP: global enterprises in high-compliance verticals: e-commerce, financial services, healthcare, telecom
- Pricing model: unknown (not public)

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Natural language; "tell Scout your goal and it drafts an agent," and Scout keeps proposing improvements.
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor SaaS; GigaML heritage offered on-prem/in-cloud LLM deployment (current self-host availability unknown).
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Unknown; channels are voice, chat, and email; no public integration catalog.
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Automatic ingestion of company policies, knowledge, and operations; RAG.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Autonomous resolution; Scout-shipped fixes require human approval ("Nothing goes live until you say so"); risky interactions route to humans.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, ISO 42001.
7. **Pricing model:** Unknown.
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led, Fortune 100 targets; DoorDash as flagship production deployment.
9. **Moat thesis:** "Emotionally intelligent" voice experience (ultra-low latency, 99 languages, sentiment/pitch/tone detection, dynamic interrupts); self-improving Scout loop; compliance certs.
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary platform; GigaML roots were fine-tuning open-source LLMs.
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflows "from live delivery issues to compliance decisions"; always-on call handling.

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end support across voice, chat, and email: emotion-aware call handling (urgency/profanity detection), personalized voices, dynamic interrupts; Scout analyzes conversations and ships KPI improvements with approval; at DoorDash it maintains a 90%+ resolve rate on live-delivery scenarios at production scale.
- **Weaknesses:** No public pricing or integration catalog; pivot from on-prem LLM infrastructure to applied voice agents required a rebuilt GTM; ~30-person team carrying a $61M raise; long enterprise sales cycles.
- **Trajectory:** From customer support toward an "enterprise operations platform"; voice-first differentiation against Sierra, Decagon, and Parloa.
- **M&A:** none visible.
