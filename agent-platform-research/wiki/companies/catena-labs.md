# Catena Labs

**Thesis (one line):** An AI-native bank providing regulated financial infrastructure and governance for autonomous agents to execute secure, policy-bound commerce payments.

## Facts

- Founded: 2022
- HQ: Boston, Massachusetts
- YC batch: none
- Backers: Acrew Capital (lead), a16z crypto (lead), Breyer Capital, General Catalyst, QED Investors, Circle Ventures, Coinbase Ventures, CoinFund
- Funding: $48M total; $30M Series A (2026-05-20)
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Businesses deploying AI agents that require autonomous payment capabilities
- Pricing model: unknown

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Dashboards and programmable financial infrastructure (APIs)
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** unknown
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary connectors; supports Mastercard Agent Pay for Machines and x402 protocol
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Immutable audit trails and first-class ledger transactions with linked evidence
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Enforces human-defined deterministic policies and approval gates
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** Verifiable agent identity, deterministic policies, audit trails, and spending limits
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** Invite-only (current status)
9. **Moat thesis:** Regulatory moat via national trust bank charter and deep integration with payment rails
10. **Open-source posture:** OSS components via Agent Commerce Kit (ACK)
11. **Task horizon:** Persistent (always-on financial infrastructure)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Agents can autonomously manage treasury, process payroll, handle procurement, and pay for compute/API access within strict guardrails.
- **Weaknesses:** High regulatory dependency on the pending OCC national trust bank charter to fully realize its "bank" value proposition.
- **Trajectory:** Moving from a protocol-based developer preview (ACK-Lab) to a fully regulated financial institution for the agent economy.
- **M&A:** none visible
