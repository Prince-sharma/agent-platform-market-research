# Unify

**Thesis (one line):** An AI-powered outbound platform using prospecting agents to automate lead discovery, research, and personalized multi-channel sequencing.

## Facts

- Founded: unknown
- HQ: unknown
- YC batch: none
- Backers: Battery Ventures (lead), OpenAI Startup Fund
- Funding: $40M total; Series B (Jul 2025)
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: GTM teams, BDRs, and sales reps at growth-stage companies
- Pricing model: Per seat monthly ($20 Base, $60 Pro) plus credit-based usage

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Natural language chat for prompt-driven outbound and list building.
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS.
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary connector library accessing 40+ data vendors and signal providers.
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Live sync with systems of record (HubSpot, Salesforce) and integrated wholesale data marketplace.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Approval gates; users review AI-generated copy and qualified lists before execution.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC, SOC 2 Type II compliance, SSO authentication, and hard spend caps on credits.
7. **Pricing model:** Platform fee per seat ($20-$60/mo) plus credits for data and LLM usage.
8. **GTM motion:** PLG self-serve (free tier) and sales-led for Business annual plans.
9. **Moat thesis:** Data gravity via 40+ vetted vendors and workflow lock-in through CRM sync.
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary.
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow (minutes to hours) for research, qualification, and sequencing.

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Agents perform end-to-end prospecting: identifying accounts via signals, pulling contacts, researching fit, and writing personalized multi-channel messages.
- **Weaknesses:** Heavy reliance on third-party data vendors; potential for "AI-sounding" outreach if not carefully reviewed.
- **Trajectory:** Moving toward a "system of action" for revenue, expanding into managed mailboxes and dialer capabilities.
- **M&A:** none visible
