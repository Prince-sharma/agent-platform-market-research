# n8n

**Thesis (one line):** An open-source workflow automation platform that blends visual logic with native AI agents for controlled enterprise process automation.

## Facts

- Founded: unknown
- HQ: unknown
- YC batch: none
- Backers: Accel, SAP
- Funding: $180M Series C, Oct 2025
- Valuation: $5.2B (May 2026)
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Technical teams and organizations requiring governed AI automation
- Pricing model: Monthly workflow executions

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Drag-and-drop canvas with natural language AI Workflow Builder
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS, self-host, and on-prem
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary connector library (500+ integrations) and MCP support
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Built-in RAG and live sync with systems of record
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Fixed workflow with approval gates and human-in-the-loop checkpoints
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC, audit trails, SOC2 compliance, and external secret store integration
7. **Pricing model:** Platform fee based on monthly workflow executions
8. **GTM motion:** PLG self-serve and sales-led for Enterprise
9. **Moat thesis:** Workflow lock-in and extensive integration ecosystem
10. **Open-source posture:** Fair-code license; self-host option available via GitHub
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow (minutes) to persistent (always-on)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Orchestrates multi-agent teams and RAG pipelines combined with deterministic rule-based logic and human approvals.
- **Weaknesses:** Complexity of visual canvas may increase for extremely large-scale logic compared to pure code.
- **Trajectory:** Strong growth evidenced by a $5.2B valuation following strategic investment from SAP in 2026.
- **M&A:** None visible
