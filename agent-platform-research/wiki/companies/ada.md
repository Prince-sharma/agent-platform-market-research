# Ada

**Thesis (one line):** Veteran AI agent platform for enterprise customer service, reborn as agentic CX with a unified reasoning engine across channels.

## Facts

- Founded: 2016 (Mike Murchison, Joe Zhou)
- HQ: Toronto
- YC batch: none
- Backers: Spark Capital (led $130M Series C, 2021), Accel (led $44M Series B, 2020), Bessemer (seed), FirstMark, Tiger Global
- Funding: $200M total; $130M Series C (May 2021); no rounds disclosed since
- Valuation: $1.2B (May 2021)
- Revenue/ARR: Undisclosed; 108% agentic ARR growth and 146% NRR (Mar 2026); third-party estimate ~$70M (low confidence)
- ICP: Enterprise customer service teams in high-volume industries (financial services, travel, SaaS, ecommerce)
- Pricing model: Platform fee plus per-resolution (third-party reported: ~$30-80K/yr platform, $1.00-3.50 per resolution)

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Config workflow with Performance Center analytics and Playbooks for structured procedures.
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS.
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary connector library (Zendesk, Salesforce) plus MCP server.
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Built-in RAG ingesting knowledge bases (GitHub, Contentful, Docusaurus, and others).
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Autonomous resolution with handoff gates to human agents via Zendesk/Salesforce.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** SOC 2 Type 2, SOC 3, HIPAA, GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA; zero data retention with LLM providers.
7. **Pricing model:** Platform fee plus per-resolution usage.
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led enterprise with ACX services layer (ACX Practice, ACX Experts).
9. **Moat thesis:** Deep integrations with legacy CX stacks, decade of enterprise deployments, Reasoning Engine IP.
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary; open APIs and SDKs.
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflows (minutes) via Playbooks.

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end resolution of complex inquiries (e.g., airline flight cancellations) using real-time data and sandboxed Python tools; unified Reasoning Engine (Feb 2026).
- **Weaknesses:** Late pivot from scripted chatbots to agentic AI; absolute ARR undisclosed; disputes over "resolution" definitions in pricing.
- **Trajectory:** Full platform shift to agentic CX; doubled YoY (Mar 2026); expanding tool use and channel coverage.
- **M&A:** None visible.
