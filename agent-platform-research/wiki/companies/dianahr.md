## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end HR operations including state compliance (Workers' Comp, disability), payroll/bill pay for domestic and international teams, onboarding/offboarding, and HRIS audits.
- **Weaknesses:** Operates in a "thin long tail" of HR agents dominated by larger players like Paradox.
