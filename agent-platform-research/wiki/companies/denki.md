# Denki

**Thesis (one line):** An AI-powered assurance platform that automates financial auditing and compliance for public companies to replace manual evidence-heavy processes.

## Facts

- Founded: 2025
- HQ: San Francisco
- YC batch: F25
- Backers: Base10 Partners (lead), Shine Capital (lead), Y Combinator, 20VC
- Funding: $4.1M, Seed, 2026-03-05
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Internal auditors and CFOs at public companies
- Pricing model: Monthly subscription (Starter $499/mo, Pro $999/mo, Enterprise custom)

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** natural language (via AI agents) and dashboards; or unknown
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** proprietary connector library (integrates with Auditboard, Workiva, ERPs, Jira)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** live sync with systems of record (org charts, processes, general ledgers)
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** approval gates (produces workpapers with full audit traces for review)
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** audit trails (full audit trace for every step)
7. **Pricing model:** per seat or platform fee plus usage (tiered monthly plans)
8. **GTM motion:** PLG self-serve (Starter/Pro plans) and sales-led (Enterprise)
9. **Moat thesis:** workflow lock-in (integration into SOX/BSA/AML compliance cycles)
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** persistent (always-on continuous assurance monitoring)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Deploys three specialized agents: Walker (control inventory mapping), Guardian (real-time control validation and HR reconciliation), and Tracer (linking ledgers and ERPs).
- **Weaknesses:** Highly niche focus on public company financial regulations may limit total addressable market compared to general AI agents.
- **Trajectory:** Moving toward a "full-stack AI audit firm" model where software performs 99% of the audit work.
- **M&A:** none visible
