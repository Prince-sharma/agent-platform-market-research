# SmarterDx

**Thesis (one line):** Clinical AI agents that analyze patient records to recover missed revenue and ensure billing accuracy for health systems.

## Facts

- Founded: 2020
- HQ: unknown
- YC batch: none
- Backers: New Mountain Capital, Bessemer Venture Partners
- Funding: $50M total raised; strategic investment by New Mountain Capital (2025)
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Hospitals and health systems (specifically CDI and coding teams)
- Pricing model: unknown

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** unknown
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** proprietary connector library (integrates with patient records/billing systems)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** live sync with systems of record (analyzes 100% of patient records at discharge)
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** approval gates (empowers CDI/coding teams to validate care prior to billing)
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** unknown
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** sales-led
9. **Moat thesis:** data gravity (proprietary clinical AI trained on patient records) and workflow lock-in
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** multi-step workflow (minutes)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Agents perform end-to-end clinical record review to identify documentation gaps, validate care delivered versus billed, and provide evidence to overturn payer denials.
- **Weaknesses:** High dependency on the quality of unstructured clinical documentation in EHRs.
- **Trajectory:** Transitioned from a standalone tool to a core component of "Smarter Technologies," a consolidated AI-enabled RCM platform.
- **M&A:** Acquired/Combined by New Mountain Capital in May 2025 to form Smarter Technologies alongside Thoughtful.ai and Access Healthcare.
