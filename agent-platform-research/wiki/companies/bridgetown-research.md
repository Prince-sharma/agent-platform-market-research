# Bridgetown Research

**Thesis (one line):** AI agents automating primary and secondary business research and due diligence to accelerate strategic decision-making for investors and executives.

## Facts

- Founded: 2023
- HQ: Seattle
- YC batch: none
- Backers: Accel (lead), Lightspeed (lead), Sequoia Capital, unnamed research university
- Funding: $19M Series A, 2025-02-26
- Valuation: $250M (2025-02-26)
- Revenue/ARR: "a few million dollars" within four months of launch (2025-02-26, low confidence)
- ICP: Private equity, venture capital, consulting firms, and corporate strategy teams
- Pricing model: Annual platform fees, variable usage fees, and pay-per-report

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** natural language
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** unknown
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** built-in RAG; gathers proprietary data via expert voice interviews and customer surveys
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** approval gates; "steerability and auditability" allowing users to trace agent reasoning and verify recordings
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** audit trails
7. **Pricing model:** platform fee plus usage
8. **GTM motion:** sales-led
9. **Moat thesis:** data gravity (proprietary primary research data)
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** multi-step workflow (minutes to 24 hours)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end due diligence: secondary research, hypothesis creation, primary data collection via AI voice interviews, synthesis into reports and presentations.
- **Weaknesses:** Potential for LLM hallucinations (mitigated by auditability and manual verification of recordings).
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from private equity deal screening into broader market research and strategic initiatives for corporates and professional services.
- **M&A:** none visible
