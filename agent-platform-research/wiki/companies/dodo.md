# Dodo

**Thesis (one line):** Autonomous AI employees handling end-to-end front- and back-office communications for specialty healthcare clinics.

## Facts

- Founded: unknown
- HQ: San Francisco
- YC batch: S24
- Backers: Y Combinator
- Funding: unknown
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Specialty clinics (starting with veterinary, expanding to dental and physical therapy)
- Pricing model: unknown

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** unknown
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** proprietary connector library (integrates directly with clinic systems)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** live sync with systems of record
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** fully autonomous; no human in the loop
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** unknown
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** sales-led (seeking introductions to clinic owners/PE)
9. **Moat thesis:** workflow lock-in via deep integration with clinic systems of record
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** multi-step workflow (minutes) to persistent (always-on)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Handles scheduling, prescription refills, medical record transmission, intake, and follow-ups via calls and texts; includes proactive outbound calls to fill cancellations.
- **Weaknesses:** High dependency on the stability and API openness of legacy clinic management systems.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from veterinary clinics into broader specialty healthcare verticals like dental and physical therapy.
- **M&A:** none visible
