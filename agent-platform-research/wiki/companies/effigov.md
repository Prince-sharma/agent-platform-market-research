# EffiGov

**Thesis (one line):** AI voice agents for local governments that automate 311 and departmental phone lines to provide 24/7 constituent services.

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** unknown
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** proprietary connector library (integrates with govtech stack like Esri, Salesforce, Tyler Technologies)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** live sync with systems of record
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** fully autonomous with "Call Oversight" safety layer for mid-call intervention and "Continuous QA" for staff-approved fixes
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** SOC 2 Type II (in progress), US-based data, zero data retention with AI model provider
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** sales-led
9. **Moat thesis:** integrations (deep govtech stack connectivity) and federated agent architecture
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** single-turn to multi-step workflow (minutes)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Deploys federated agents (one per department) that share context to resolve inquiries, log tickets (e.g., potholes), or route calls to staff.
- **Weaknesses:** Traction remains early; limited to a small number of case studies.
- **Trajectory:** Moving toward becoming an "AI OS for Cities" by expanding from simple call answering to a federated system of departmental agents.
- **M&A:** none visible
