# 11x

**Thesis (one line):** AI digital workers for outbound (Alice) and inbound (Julian) sales prospecting and meeting booking.

## Facts

- Founded: unknown
- HQ: San Francisco, CA / London, UK
- YC batch: none
- Backers: a16z (lead), Benchmark
- Funding: $74M+ total; $50M Series B (2024)
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Sales, marketing, and RevOps teams
- Pricing model: Per lead; Growth plan starts at $3,750/mo ($36k/year)

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Natural language and config workflow for digital workers; or unknown
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary connectors for Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho, Slack, G2, and a REST API
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Live sync with CRM as system of record; includes bundled contact data and research
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Fully autonomous prospecting and booking with exclusion rules based on CRM account ownership
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC, SSO, Custom DPA, Custom SLA, and granular read/write CRM permissions
7. **Pricing model:** Platform fee based on lead volume (Growth: $3,750/mo; Pro/Enterprise: custom)
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led with published entry-tier pricing
9. **Moat thesis:** Workflow lock-in via deep CRM bi-directional sync and managed email deliverability infrastructure
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** Long-horizon (multi-step outbound sequences and real-time inbound qualification)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end sales pipeline management: Alice handles outbound research and booking; Julian handles real-time inbound qualification via phone, chat, and SMS.
- **Weaknesses:** Significant reputational damage following a 2025 customer-claims scandal and the subsequent exit of CEO Hasan Sukkar.
- **Trajectory:** Shifting from aggressive growth claims to a more stabilized "digital worker" positioning after a public implosion.
- **M&A:** none visible
