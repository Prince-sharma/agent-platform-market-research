# Careforce

**Thesis (one line):** Autonomous AI workers for healthcare organizations to handle patient outreach, scheduling, and care coordination without requiring system integrations.

## Facts

- Founded: unknown
- HQ: unknown
- YC batch: F24
- Backers: Y Combinator
- Funding: unknown
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Health centers, practices, digital health/CCM companies, and payers
- Pricing model: unknown

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** unknown
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** proprietary; claims "no integration" required as agents navigate tools like staff would
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** live sync with systems of record (EMR, scheduling, and payer portals)
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** fully autonomous for outreach and scheduling; unknown guardrails
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** unknown
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** sales-led (targeting healthcare orgs)
9. **Moat thesis:** workflow lock-in via "human-glue" task automation and deep healthcare domain expertise
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** multi-step workflow (minutes to hours) for patient outreach and booking

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end patient coordination: identifying unscheduled patients, performing multi-channel outreach (phone, text, email, mail) in 29 languages, and booking appointments directly into portals.
- **Weaknesses:** High reliance on the ability to navigate UI/portals without APIs may face stability issues if portal interfaces change.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from simple scheduling to broader administrative "human-glue" tasks and revenue recovery for healthcare providers.
- **M&A:** none visible
