# Edra

**Thesis (one line):** Edra turns operational data into executable knowledge, allowing AI agents to automate enterprise workflows based on actual business processes.

## Facts

- Founded: 2024
- HQ: New York
- YC batch: none
- Backers: Sequoia (lead), 8VC, A*, Hubspot Ventures
- Funding: $30M Series A, 2026-03-18
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Enterprises with operationally complex organizations (e.g., IT service management, technical support)
- Pricing model: Paid plans only

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Dashboards for visibility into executable knowledge and process discovery; unknown if SDKs exist.
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** unknown
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary connectors to source systems including ServiceNow, Zendesk, Outlook, and other common source systems.
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Live sync with systems of record; reverse-engineers tickets, logs, and messages into a "living knowledge base" of executable instructions.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Provides "white-box" transparent instructions; agents execute reliably following codified processes.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** Auditable instructions; unknown regarding RBAC or spend caps.
7. **Pricing model:** Paid plans only; specific units unknown.
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led; offers demos and targets industry leaders.
9. **Moat thesis:** Data gravity and workflow lock-in via the creation of a proprietary, self-improving "living playbook" of company-specific tribal knowledge.
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow (minutes) to long-horizon (hours to weeks) for operational automation.

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Agents can end-to-end automate IT service management and technical support by following reverse-engineered SOPs derived from historical data.
- **Weaknesses:** High dependency on the quality and availability of existing system data (tickets, logs, messages) for initial process discovery.
- **Trajectory:** Moving toward "self-improving" agents that continuously update their knowledge library as teams work and processes evolve.
- **M&A:** none visible
