# Assemble

**Thesis (one line):** AI coworkers for IT teams that automate configuration and development within core enterprise systems like ERP, CRM, and HRIS.

## Facts

- Founded: 2026
- HQ: San Francisco
- YC batch: Summer 2026
- Backers: Y Combinator (Partner: Ankit Gupta)
- Funding: unknown
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: IT teams, Business Systems teams, and Enterprise Applications leads at scaling companies
- Pricing model: unknown

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** natural language
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** unknown
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** proprietary connectors for ERP, CRM, and HRIS (e.g., Salesforce, SAP, Workday, NetSuite)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** live sync with systems of record
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** approval gates; users review, approve, and can roll back changes
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** audit trails (agent trace), snapshots for restoration
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** unknown
9. **Moat thesis:** unknown
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** long-horizon (hours to weeks)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Performs end-to-end development work inside enterprise systems, including scoping dependencies, building logic (e.g., APEX automation), migrating data between platforms, and validating changes.
- **Weaknesses:** No visible traction; operating in a highly consolidated market where suites (e.g., ServiceNow) are acquiring vertical agents.
- **Trajectory:** Positioning as a "safe-execution" layer for IT transformation projects to reduce backlogs.
- **M&A:** none visible
