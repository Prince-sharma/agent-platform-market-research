# Rebolt

**Thesis (one line):** AI agents automating restaurant management and supplier coordination to reduce staffing burdens and recover lost revenue from delivery fraud.

## Facts

- Founded: 2025
- HQ: unknown
- YC batch: W25
- Backers: Y Combinator
- Funding: unknown
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Restaurant owners and managers
- Pricing model: Credit model for builders; Pro/Builder and Enterprise plans (estimated $20-$25/month for basic)

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Natural language ("vibecoding") via prompts to create agents and apps.
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS.
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary connector library compatible with CRM systems, databases, file stores, and APIs.
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Live sync with systems of record via "Holocron," a live operating model connecting business objects (orders, invoices, etc.).
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Approval gates (e.g., approvals and exceptions handled in manufacturing/construction workflows) and test sandboxes/pilots.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC (roles and permissions), security and governance layer, and audit logging for supplier communications.
7. **Pricing model:** Platform fee (Pro/Builder plans) plus usage-based builder credits.
8. **GTM motion:** Hybrid; PLG self-serve ("Start for free") and sales-led for Enterprise.
9. **Moat thesis:** Workflow lock-in through the "Holocron" operating model and deep vertical integration into restaurant ops (delivery app portals).
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary.
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow (e.g., identifying missing items, comparing photos, and automatically disputing refunds).

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end automation of restaurant operations including delivery app refund disputes (using image verification), centralized analytics, AI-driven candidate screening/hiring, and autonomous supplier communication.
- **Weaknesses:** High dependency on third-party delivery app portal stability and API access for dispute automation.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from a niche restaurant tool into a broader "AI-Native Enterprise Operations Platform" targeting manufacturing, construction, architecture, and automotive sectors.
- **M&A:** None visible.
