# Rox

**Thesis (one line):** An AI revenue operating system using agent swarms to automate GTM workflows on top of enterprise CRM and data warehouse signals.

## Facts

- Founded: unknown
- HQ: unknown
- YC batch: none
- Backers: unknown
- Funding: unknown
- Valuation: $1.2B (2026-03-12)
- Revenue/ARR: $8M ARR (Mar 2026, high confidence)
- ICP: Global 2000 enterprise sales teams and strategic reps
- Pricing model: Usage-based "Agent Actions" (Starter: Free; Core: from $50/mo; Enterprise: Custom)

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Natural language via "Command" conversational interface across web, Slack, macOS, and iOS.
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS (cloud-only); built on AWS.
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary connectors for Salesforce, HubSpot, Gmail, Outlook, Slack, M365, and warehouse-native (Snowflake, Redshift, BigQuery); supports MCP.
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Unified governed knowledge graph consolidating CRM, finance, support, product telemetry, and web data.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Multi-agent orchestration with guardrail approvals for tool calls; every suggestion is explainable and reversible.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC, audit logs, rate limits, and a multi-layer safety architecture for legal and business compliance.
7. **Pricing model:** Consumption-based "Agent Actions" (e.g., Core tiers at 10k/20k/40k/100k actions/mo) and custom annual enterprise contracts.
8. **GTM motion:** Hybrid; PLG self-serve for Starter/Core, but primarily sales-led for Global 2000 Enterprise tier.
9. **Moat thesis:** Data gravity via unified knowledge graph and workflow lock-in through deep CRM/warehouse integration.
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary.
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflows (minutes to hours) including research, outreach, and proposal generation.

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Orchestrates "agent swarms" to handle end-to-end prospecting, account research, meeting transcription, outreach, and pipeline management.
- **Weaknesses:** Variable consumption pricing makes monthly spend hard to forecast; full warehouse-native value locked behind custom enterprise contracts.
- **Trajectory:** Moving toward a complete "revenue operating system" that replaces passive CRM record-keeping with active AI execution.
- **M&A:** Acquired Quilt (2025-09-23).
