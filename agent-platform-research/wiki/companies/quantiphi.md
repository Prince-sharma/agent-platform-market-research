## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Config workflow via modular components (Qompositor, Neqsus, Qalibrate, Qodex)
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Dedicated VPC (AWS/GCP), on-premises, or behind firewalls
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary connector library (blinq APIs) for Dialogue, Speech, Search, and RPA
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Built-in RAG via Qompositor for embedding extraction and vector DB storage
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Multi-step autonomous reasoning with Responsible AI guardrails for groundedness and safety
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC, SOC 2, and HIPAA compliance; enterprise data ownership of models and data
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** Services-led transformation augmented by productized platform IP (TSaaS)
9. **Moat thesis:** Domain-specific fine-tuning (Qalibrate) and deep cloud ecosystem partnerships (AWS, GCP, NVIDIA)
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary; supports deployment in customer-owned cloud environments
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow (minutes) to persistent (24/7 operational capability)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end automation of software engineering (Codeaira), document processing (Dociphi), and enterprise search/synthesis (baioniq).
- **Weaknesses:** Heavy reliance on cloud partner ecosystems (AWS/GCP) for deployment and distribution.
- **Trajectory:** Shifting from traditional AI services toward a "Technology Services as Software" (TSaaS) model.
- **M&A:** Acquired Candyspace to enhance AI-native digital experience capabilities.
