# Atlassian Rovo

**Thesis (one line):** AI agents and knowledge layer integrated across Atlassian's suite to automate workflows and unify organizational data.

## Facts

- Founded: 2002 (Atlassian)
- HQ: Sydney
- YC batch: none
- Backers: public company (TEAM)
- Funding: public company (TEAM)
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Enterprise teams using Jira, Confluence, and Jira Service Management
- Pricing model: bundled with cloud subscriptions; consumption-based credits

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** natural language and low-code Studio (manual configuration)
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** MCP (Model Context Protocol) and proprietary connectors
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** live sync with systems of record (Atlassian and 3rd party apps)
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** approval gates (tools require confirmation)
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC (respects existing user permissions), verified business domains
7. **Pricing model:** platform fee (bundled) plus usage (pooled Rovo credits per seat)
8. **GTM motion:** PLG self-serve and bundle distribution to installed base
9. **Moat thesis:** distribution, data gravity, and workflow lock-in
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** multi-step workflow (minutes)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Agents can generate/refine content, clean Jira backlogs, organize Confluence pages, and execute specialized skills via tools.
- **Weaknesses:** Dependency on Atlassian Cloud ecosystem for full utility.
- **Trajectory:** Moving toward a comprehensive "AI teammate" model with expanded MCP support for third-party extensibility.
- **M&A:** none visible
