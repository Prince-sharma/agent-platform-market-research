# Conversica

**Thesis (one line):** AI revenue digital assistants that automate lead engagement and qualification via omnichannel conversations to drive sales pipeline.

## Facts

- Founded: 2007
- HQ: unknown
- YC batch: none
- Backers: Morgan Stanley Expansion Capital (lead)
- Funding: Majority recapitalization (Jul 2026)
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Revenue teams in automotive, retail, and enterprise sectors with large lead databases.
- Pricing model: Volume-based; base plan starts at $2,999/month billed annually.

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Configurable conversation flows with "Build Your AI Agent" scaffolding for goals, skills, and guardrails.
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS.
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary connectors for Salesforce, HubSpot, MAP, CDP, and ABM tools.
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Built-in knowledge engine for FAQs and policies; real-time CRM sync for contact/account records.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Autonomous lead nurture with defined guardrails for tone, timing, and opt-out logic.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** Established compliance frameworks, procurement-friendly credentials, and defined policy guardrails.
7. **Pricing model:** Platform fee starting at $2,999/month; volume-based rather than per-seat.
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led enterprise motion with complex implementation ($5k-$15k fees).
9. **Moat thesis:** 15+ years of enterprise deployment experience and data from 1.5 billion conversations.
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary.
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow (minutes to days) for lead follow-up and qualification.

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end lead engagement including email/SMS nurture, intent interpretation, and CRM-triggered actions like meeting scheduling.
- **Weaknesses:** Lacks native phone/dialer and LinkedIn automation; some users report scripted or inflexible responses.
- **Trajectory:** Shifting toward "AI-Native Revenue Activation" with the launch of the Ignite engine for specific verticals like automotive.
- **M&A:** Majority recapitalization led by Morgan Stanley Expansion Capital (Jul 2026).
