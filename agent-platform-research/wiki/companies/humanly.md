# Humanly

**Thesis (one line):** AI agents for high-volume and frontline hiring that automate the end-to-end recruitment lifecycle to accelerate time-to-hire and improve retention.

## Facts

- Founded: unknown
- HQ: Seattle
- YC batch: none
- Backers: SEEK Investments (lead), MassMutual Catalyst Fund, Drive Capital, Zeal Capital Partners
- Funding: $25M Series B (2026-05-21)
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Mid-market and Fortune 500 companies in healthcare, retail, logistics, hospitality, and customer support
- Pricing model: Outcome-based (pay-per-candidate and pay-per-hire options)

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Config workflow (Interview Builder to customize questions, depth, tone, and persona)
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary connector library (embeds directly into ATS workflows)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Built-in RAG (trained on 4M+ interviews and behavioral science to capture hiring signals)
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Fully autonomous screening and scheduling with human-in-the-loop for final hiring decisions and relationship building
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** Standardized scoring models to reduce bias and ensure defensible hiring decisions
7. **Pricing model:** Per outcome (pay-per-candidate and pay-per-hire)
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led
9. **Moat thesis:** Data gravity (trained on millions of real candidate conversations) and workflow lock-in via ATS integration
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow (engages, screens, interviews, and schedules candidates)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end recruitment automation including 24/7 candidate engagement (AI Recruiter), structured interviewing across chat, voice, and video (AI Interviewer), CRM talent pool management, and ATS pipeline orchestration.
- **Weaknesses:** Operates in a "thin long tail" HR vertical dominated by larger players like Paradox.
- **Trajectory:** Shifting from a software-as-a-service (SaaS) model to a "service-as-software" model where the company delivers hiring outcomes rather than just providing tools.
- **M&A:** None visible
