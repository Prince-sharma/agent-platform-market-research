# PolyAI

**Thesis (one line):** Enterprise-grade voice AI platform providing lifelike, low-latency agents for complex customer service conversations at scale.

## Facts

- Founded: 2017
- HQ: unknown
- YC batch: none
- Backers: Georgian (lead), Hedosophia (lead), Khosla Ventures (lead), NVentures, British Business Bank, Citi Ventures, Squarepoint Ventures, Sands Capital, Zendesk Ventures, Point72 Ventures
- Funding: $200M+ total; $86M Series D (2025-12-15)
- Valuation: $750M (2025-12-15)
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Large enterprises in financial services, healthcare, hospitality, insurance, energy, and retail
- Pricing model: Per-minute basis

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Code (ADK, CLI, Git-backed) and Agent Studio (UI)
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Deep API integrations for real-time backend calls
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Live sync with systems of record via custom functions
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Fixed workflow with business logic owned by user; guardrails via enterprise controls
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** Compliance certificates, regular audits, 24/7 data infrastructure, and RBAC
7. **Pricing model:** Platform fee (implied) plus per-minute usage
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led
9. **Moat thesis:** Proprietary ASR and TTS engines for ultra-low latency and lifelike quality
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow (minutes)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Handles end-to-end customer calls including authentication, order status lookups, and payment refunds via API integrations.
- **Weaknesses:** unknown
- **Trajectory:** Moving toward the "agentic enterprise" where AI detects and responds to problems in real time across the organization.
- **M&A:** none visible
