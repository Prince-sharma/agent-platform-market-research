2. **Runtime and tenancy:** vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** proprietary connector library (integrated EHR/CRM/RCM stack)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** live sync with systems of record (built-in AI-native EHR)
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** approval gates (collaboration between AI workers and clinical teams)
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** unknown
7. **Pricing model:** unknown

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end clinic operations: Sarah handles inbound calls, insurance verification, and booking; Emma automates clinical documentation; Eric manages coding, claims submission, and denial management.
- **Weaknesses:** High friction for customer acquisition due to the requirement of replacing the entire clinic software stack.
- **Trajectory:** Moving from point-solution AI assistants to a comprehensive "AI-native OS" for behavioral health.
