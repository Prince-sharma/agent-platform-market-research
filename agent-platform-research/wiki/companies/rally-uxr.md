# Rally UXR

**Thesis (one line):** Agentic user research infrastructure that automates participant recruitment, scheduling, and incentives to scale enterprise research with built-in governance.

## Facts

- Founded: 2021
- HQ: New York City, NY
- YC batch: W22
- Backers: Canapi Ventures (lead), Stage 2 Capital, Y Combinator, TIA Ventures
- Funding: $20.3M total, Series A, 2025-06-02
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: 3x year-over-year growth (reported 2025-06-02), confidence: medium
- ICP: Enterprise research, product, and design teams
- Pricing model: unknown

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** natural language (via Rally Agent) and config workflow
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** MCP (Claude, Cursor, Slack) and proprietary connectors (Salesforce, Qualtrics)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** live sync with systems of record (Salesforce) and built-in participant CRM
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** fully autonomous recruitment/scheduling with governance guardrails
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC, SOC 2, GDPR, CCPA, contact limits, and participation cooldowns
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** sales-led (demo-based)
9. **Moat thesis:** data gravity (participant panels) and workflow lock-in (governance/compliance)
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** multi-step workflow (minutes to days for recruitment/scheduling)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end automation of the research operational layer: finding participants, screening, outreach, scheduling, and incentive distribution.
- **Weaknesses:** unknown
- **Trajectory:** Moving from a specialized Research CRM to a broader "agentic infrastructure" that integrates directly into developer and product tools via MCP.
- **M&A:** none visible
