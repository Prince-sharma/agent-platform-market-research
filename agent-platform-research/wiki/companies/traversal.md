# Traversal

**Thesis (one line):** An AI SRE platform using causal ML to autonomously diagnose and resolve complex enterprise production incidents, reducing MTTR.

## Facts

- Founded: 2025 (emerged from stealth June 2025)
- HQ: New York City
- YC batch: none
- Backers: Sequoia (lead), Kleiner Perkins (lead), Amex Ventures
- Funding: $48M - $53M total; last round included $5M strategic investment from Amex Ventures (2026-03-04)
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Global financial institutions and Fortune 100 enterprises with complex, distributed infrastructure
- Pricing model: unknown

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** unknown
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** flexible deployment model; security-first architecture for highly regulated industries
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** proprietary; integrates across siloed observability stacks (e.g., Splunk, Datadog) to analyze telemetry data
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Production World Model™ (continuously learning machine-readable model of production environment) and Causal Search Engine™
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** agentic incident response; agents work alongside engineers to diagnose and eventually auto-remediate
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** security-first architecture designed for highly regulated industries
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** sales-led (strategic partnerships with enterprise customers like American Express)
9. **Moat thesis:** Causal machine learning (root cause vs. correlation) and the Production World Model™
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** multi-step workflow (detect, troubleshoot, and resolve incidents)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Agents autonomously detect, troubleshoot, and resolve production incidents by analyzing petabytes of telemetry data. They use causal reasoning to identify root causes rather than simple correlations and can provide validated next steps or perform auto-remediation.
- **Weaknesses:** High dependency on the quality of existing enterprise observability data; faces competition from massive suites (e.g., ServiceNow) and well-funded independents (e.g., Resolve).
- **Trajectory:** Aiming to define the "operational intelligence" category for enterprise infrastructure, specifically targeting global financial institutions in 2026.
- **M&A:** none visible
