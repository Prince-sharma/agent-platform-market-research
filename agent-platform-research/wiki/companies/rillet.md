# Rillet

**Thesis (one line):** An AI-native ERP platform that automates accounting and finance operations to enable a continuous, zero-day close for software companies.

## Facts

- Founded: unknown
- HQ: San Francisco, CA
- YC batch: none
- Backers: ICONIQ (lead), Andreessen Horowitz, Sequoia, Bain Capital Ventures, Oak HC/FT, Battery Ventures, FirstMark, Scale Venture Partners, Creandum
- Funding: >$200M total, $100M Series C (August 2026)
- Valuation: $1B (August 2026)
- Revenue/ARR: unknown (reported doubling of new ARR in last three months as of August 2026)
- ICP: Finance-led SaaS and software companies from Series A to pre-IPO ($5M to $250M revenue)
- Pricing model: Quote-based annual SaaS subscription (not per seat or revenue-based)

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** unknown
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** proprietary connector library (native integrations with Salesforce, Brex, etc.)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** live sync with systems of record (real-time general ledger)
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** approval gates (AI agents operate in ledger with human approval where needed)
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** audit trails
7. **Pricing model:** platform fee plus usage (quote-based annual subscription)
8. **GTM motion:** sales-led (CPA-led implementation)
9. **Moat thesis:** data gravity (system of record for GL) and workflow lock-in
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** multi-step workflow (continuous transaction processing)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Agents automate data ingestion from external sources and operate directly within the general ledger to manage books and reporting.
- **Weaknesses:** Limited capabilities in inventory, warehouse, and order management compared to legacy ERPs.
- **Trajectory:** Moving toward "Accounting Superintelligence" by vertically integrating the finance stack.
- **M&A:** none visible
