# UiPath Agentic Automation

**Thesis (one line):** RPA suite repositioned around agents and Agent Builder to turn reasoning into reliable, governed enterprise work.

## Facts

- Founded: 2005
- HQ: New York City
- YC batch: none
- Backers: public company (PATH)
- Funding: public company (PATH)
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Enterprise organizations with mission-critical business processes
- Pricing model: Unified Pricing (Platform Units) or Flex (Agent Units)

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** drag-and-drop canvas (Canvas view) and configuration-driven (Form view)
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** vendor multi-tenant SaaS (Automation Cloud)
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** proprietary connector library (automations, activities, other agents)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** built-in RAG (Context Grounding indexes)
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** approval gates (Escalation nodes) and deterministic execution
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC, audit trails, observability, evals, and policy-driven behavior
7. **Pricing model:** platform fee plus usage (Platform Units / Agent Units)
8. **GTM motion:** sales-led
9. **Moat thesis:** distribution (installed base), workflow lock-in, and enterprise governance
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary; supports third-party coding agents (Claude Code, Cursor)
11. **Task horizon:** multi-step workflow (minutes) to long-horizon (via Maestro Case Management)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end automation from ticket classification and document extraction to complex business orchestration via Maestro Flow and Case.
- **Weaknesses:** Transitioning legacy RPA customers to a new agentic paradigm may create friction.
- **Trajectory:** Moving toward "Business Orchestration" by combining BPMN, AI agents, and RPA into a single governed layer.
- **M&A:** none visible
