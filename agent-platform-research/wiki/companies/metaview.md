# Metaview

**Thesis (one line):** An AI-native recruiting platform that transforms interview conversations into structured intelligence to remove hiring toil and improve decision-making.

## Facts

- Founded: unknown
- HQ: London and San Francisco
- YC batch: none
- Backers: GV (lead), Plural, Vertex Ventures, Seedcamp, Coelius Capital, True Equity, Victor Riparbelli, Barney Hussey-Yeo
- Funding: $50M total; $35M Series B (Jun 2025)
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Recruiting teams and Talent Ops at companies (e.g., Deel, Brex, Deliveroo, Quora)
- Pricing model: Tiered monthly subscription (Free, Pro, Max, Enterprise) based on sourcing volume

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Natural language and automated capture (AI agents for notes, job posts, and answers)
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary connector library (integrates with ATS like Workday, video tools, calendars, and SSO)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Live sync with systems of record (ATS integrations) and compounding intelligence from captured interview conversations
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Fixed workflow for capture/drafting with human review (e.g., drafting job posts and notes for recruiter use)
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** Enterprise security features and RBAC (implied by Enterprise tier)
7. **Pricing model:** Platform fee (monthly subscription) based on usage (profiles sourced)
8. **GTM motion:** PLG self-serve (Free tier available) and sales-led (Enterprise tier)
9. **Moat thesis:** Data gravity (compounding intelligence from millions of conversations) and workflow lock-in via ATS integrations
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow (minutes to hours for note generation and job post drafting)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Provides a suite of four agents: AI Notetaker (structures interview notes), AI Job Posts (drafts descriptions from intake calls), AI Answers (provides candidate/process context), and AI Reports (funnel optimization).
- **Weaknesses:** Operates in a "thin long tail" HR market dominated by larger players like Paradox.
- **Trajectory:** Moving from a specialized notetaker to a full "Agentic Recruiting Platform" that aims to recognize talent better than humans by compounding conversation data.
- **M&A:** None visible.
