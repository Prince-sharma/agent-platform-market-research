# Windsurf

**Thesis (one line):** An agentic AI code editor and enterprise platform acquired by Cognition to accelerate software engineering through integrated AI capabilities.

## Facts

- Founded: unknown
- HQ: unknown
- YC batch: none
- Backers: General Catalyst (led), Kleiner Perkins, Greenoaks
- Funding: $150M Series C 2025 (GC-led); $243M total raised per Reuters
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Software engineers and enterprise engineering teams
- Pricing model: Tiered subscription with daily/weekly quotas and usage-based overages

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Agentic IDE (integrated development environment)
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Multi-tenant SaaS (Devin Cloud) and dedicated deployment option for Enterprise
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** unknown
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Features "Memories" and "Cascade" for AI flow
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Agentic AI coding with "Windsurf Tab" passive experience and active agent usage
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** SAML/OIDC SSO, centralized enterprise admin controls, and admin dashboard with analytics
7. **Pricing model:** Per seat/plan: Free ($0), Pro ($20/mo), Max ($200/mo), Teams ($80/mo base + $40/mo per full dev seat), and custom Enterprise
8. **GTM motion:** PLG self-serve (Free/Pro/Max/Teams) and sales-led (Enterprise)
9. **Moat thesis:** Integration of agentic capabilities into the IDE workflow and proprietary SWE-1 model family
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary; offers access to leading open source models in Pro plan
11. **Task horizon:** unknown

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Agents handle the entire software engineering process beyond simple coding. They utilize a family of frontier models (SWE-1, SWE-1-lite, SWE-1-mini) to provide tool-call reasoning and passive completions.
- **Weaknesses:** High dependency on parent company Cognition's integration roadmap following the 2025 acquisition.
- **Trajectory:** Moving toward deep integration with Cognition's Devin agent to build a comprehensive future of software engineering.
- **M&A:** Acquired by Cognition on 2025-07-14; previously involved in a $2.4 billion talent/licensing deal with Google.
