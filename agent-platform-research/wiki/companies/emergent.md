# Emergent

**Thesis (one line):** An AI agent platform enabling users to build full-stack web and mobile applications from natural language ideas.

## Facts

- Founded: 2024
- HQ: Bengaluru and San Francisco
- YC batch: S24
- Backers: Creaegis (lead), MNI Ventures-Claypond, Sentinel Global, Khosla Ventures, SoftBank’s Vision Fund 2, Lightspeed, Y Combinator
- Funding: $230M total; $130M Series C (2026-07-15)
- Valuation: $1.5 billion (2026-07-15)
- Revenue/ARR: $120 million ARR (2026-07-15, high confidence)
- ICP: Solo builders, startup teams, agencies, and business owners
- Pricing model: Credit-based subscription

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Natural language prompts for app generation and a no-code AI agent builder.
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Multi-tenant SaaS with an Enterprise option for VPC setup (deploy apps in own cloud).
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** GitHub integration; agents can connect to apps and data to use tools.
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Self-hosted database support for Enterprise users.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Autonomous systems that plan, act, and adjust; agents are active processes rather than simple chat prompts.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC, audit logs, SSO, and user-level credit limits.
7. **Pricing model:** Monthly credits: Free ($0), Standard ($20/mo, 100 credits), Pro ($200/mo, 750 credits), Team ($300/mo, 1,250 shared credits).
8. **GTM motion:** PLG self-serve (Free/Standard/Pro tiers) and sales-led (Custom Enterprise).
9. **Moat thesis:** unknown
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary; offers self-hosted database support for Enterprise.
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step work; agents plan steps behind a goal and complete tasks like research and scheduling.

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Agents perform end-to-end software engineering, including natural language app generation, frontend and backend development, testing, debugging, and cloud deployment. They function as autonomous systems that reason, plan, and execute multi-step workflows.
- **Weaknesses:** Users report unpredictable costs from errors and a lack of transparency and control in the credit system.
- **Trajectory:** Rapidly scaling from launch to unicorn status in one year, targeting $500 million ARR within 12–15 months.
- **M&A:** none visible
