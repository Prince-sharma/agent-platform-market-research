# Geordie

**Thesis (one line):** A security and governance layer providing visibility, posture management, and real-time controls for autonomous enterprise AI agents.

## Facts

- Founded: 2025
- HQ: London
- YC batch: none
- Backers: Balderton Capital (lead), General Catalyst, Ten Eleven Ventures, Crosspoint Capital
- Funding: $36.5M total, $30M Series A (May 2026)
- Valuation: $155M post-money (May 2026)
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Security and IT teams at enterprises deploying autonomous AI agents
- Pricing model: unknown

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Dashboard and automated discovery across cloud, code, and endpoints; no reconfiguration of agents required.
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Multi-tenant SaaS (available via AWS Marketplace).
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Vendor agnostic; maps MCP connections, permissions, and system access.
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Live sync with systems of record to maintain a continuously updated inventory of agent configurations and activity.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Provides "Beam," a real-time remediation layer for deterministic controls and contextual interventions.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC, continuous audit trails, anomaly detection, and compliance mapping (SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, EU AI Act, NIST, OWASP).
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led with presence in UK and US; available via AWS Marketplace.
9. **Moat thesis:** Deep integration into agentic infrastructure (posture management + real-time remediation) and compliance alignment.
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary.
11. **Task horizon:** Persistent (always-on observability and governance of autonomous agents).

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Enables enterprises to discover all active agents, map their tool/data access, and apply real-time security interventions.
- **Weaknesses:** Relatively new market entrant (founded 2025) facing competition from other emerging L1 security players like Manifold and Qevlar.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding US operations and engineering teams to scale its "agent-native" security platform.
- **M&A:** none visible
