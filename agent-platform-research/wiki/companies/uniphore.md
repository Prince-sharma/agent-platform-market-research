# Uniphore

**Thesis (one line):** Conversational-AI veteran ($2.5B valuation, ~$980M raised) rebuilt as a sovereign "Business AI Cloud" spanning data, knowledge, models, and agents beyond the contact center.

## Facts

- Founded: 2008 (IIT Madras origins)
- HQ: Palo Alto, CA
- YC batch: none
- Backers: NVIDIA, AMD, Snowflake, Databricks (Series F leads, Oct 2025), NEA (Series E lead), March Capital, Sorenson Capital, John Chambers, Kris Gopalakrishnan
- Funding: ~$980M total; last round Series F $260M (Oct 2025, led by NVIDIA, AMD, Snowflake, Databricks)
- Valuation: $2.5B (Oct 2025, Series F) — flat versus $2.5B at Series E (Feb 2022)
- Revenue/ARR: unknown (not disclosed)
- ICP: Fortune 500 and large global enterprises in regulated industries (financial services, healthcare, insurance) plus BPOs; expanding to marketing, sales, HR
- Pricing model: Enterprise SaaS subscription (since 2015); no public pricing

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** No-code natural-language agent builder with drag-and-drop visual canvas; Unified Conversation Designer; BPMN-compatible process design
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** "Sovereign" deployment model — multi-tenant SaaS, dedicated cloud (AWS US and Europe), on-premises, multi-cloud; positioned for data sovereignty
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Composable architecture with 100+ enterprise connectors (SAP, Salesforce, Oracle, ServiceNow); MCP for third-party agent integration
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Zero-copy data fabric that queries data where it lives (from ActionIQ and Infoworks acquisitions); "SLM factory" distills 80-100B-parameter LLMs into 7-8B domain-specific small models via a continuous learning loop
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Agents reason, plan, and execute multi-step tasks autonomously; deterministic execution for regulated workflows; Agentic Process Discovery uses computer vision to auto-map workflows from observed behavior
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** ISO 27001:2022, SOC 2 Type 2, PCI DSS Level 1; AES-256, TLS 1.2+; built-in guardrails, policy control, RBAC, audit trails, observability, adversarial prompt defense
7. **Pricing model:** Enterprise subscription; no public unit pricing
8. **GTM motion:** Direct enterprise sales plus global SIs — Tech Mahindra "Agentic AI Factory" (Aug 2026), KPMG (Jan 2026), Cognizant (Feb 2026), Rackspace (Mar 2026)
9. **Moat thesis:** 17 years of conversational AI IP (35+ filed patents), sovereign deployment option, SLM factory economics, zero-copy data layer, AI-chip and data-stack investors as channel
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary platform; interoperable with open- and closed-source models
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step business workflows (minutes to hours); positioning toward long-horizon cross-functional automation ("agentic enterprise")

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Business AI Suite ships prebuilt agents for customer service (real-time guidance, self-service, conversation insights, recording), sales (Sales Interaction Agent), marketing (CDP agent), and HR (recruiting), with multi-agent orchestration across them
- **Weaknesses:** Serial-acquirer integration risk (seven-plus deals since 2021); broad "business AI" positioning blurs the CX wedge against focused rivals; revenue undisclosed; Series F valuation flat since Feb 2022
- **Trajectory:** Contact-center conversational AI, then Business AI Cloud (Jun 2025), then Marketing AI and Agentic AI Factory (Aug 2026); Gartner MQ CDP Leader (Jan 2026) via ActionIQ; infrastructure investors signal full-stack enterprise AI ambition
- **M&A:** Emotion Research Lab (2021), Jacada (2021), Red Box and Hexagone (2023), ActionIQ and Infoworks (Dec 2024), Orby AI and Autonom8 (Aug 2025) — a consolidation roll-up
