# Cohere Health

**Thesis (one line):** AI-driven clinical intelligence platform automating prior authorization and utilization management for health plans to reduce administrative costs and provider friction.

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** config workflow (configurable solutions, medical policy codification)
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** vendor multi-tenant SaaS (flexible deployment: in-house, delegated, or hybrid)
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** proprietary connector library (omnichannel intake: EHR, fax, app; FHIR APIs)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** built-in RAG (clinician-trained AI extracting indications from unstructured attachments)
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** approval gates (real-time approvals for routine cases; clinician review for complex requests)
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC, audit trails, compliance certs (CMS-0057-F compliant)
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** sales-led (enterprise partnerships with health plans like Humana and Geisinger)
9. **Moat thesis:** data gravity (built on 15M+ real health plan decisions) and workflow lock-in
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** multi-step workflow (minutes)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Automates the end-to-end prior authorization lifecycle: digitizing omnichannel intake, performing real-time requirement checks, extracting clinical evidence from unstructured documents to satisfy policy, and providing AI-guided summaries for human clinical reviewers.
- **Weaknesses:** High dependency on the accuracy of medical policy codification; potential resistance from providers if AI-driven denials increase.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from prior authorization into a broader "cost containment spectrum," including payment integrity, clinical coding validation, and appeals.
- **M&A:** none visible
