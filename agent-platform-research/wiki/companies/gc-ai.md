# GC AI

**Thesis (one line):** AI workspace for in-house counsel automating contract review, legal research, and workflow orchestration via specialized legal agents.

## Facts

- Founded: unknown
- HQ: unknown
- YC batch: none
- Backers: Scale Venture (lead), Northzone
- Funding: $60M total; $60M Series B (Nov 2025)
- Valuation: $550M - $555M (Nov 2025)
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: In-house legal teams and solo/fractional General Counsel
- Pricing model: Individual ($500/month); Team/Enterprise (on request)

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Natural language chat, Microsoft Word add-in, and "Easy Edit" browser-based document editor.
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS with segregated databases and AES-256 encryption.
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Agent Connectors for 20+ apps including Gmail, Outlook, Slack, Drive, and HubSpot.
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Multi-model RAG (5 models) with built-in US Case Law (13M+ opinions) and user-uploaded files.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Approval gates for agent actions; "Exact Quote" citations for human verification of legal analysis.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** SOC 2 Type II, RBAC (SSO for teams), audit trails, and no model training on user data.
7. **Pricing model:** Per seat (monthly/yearly) for users; credit-based billing for API usage.
8. **GTM motion:** Hybrid PLG (14-day free trial, self-serve individual seats) and sales-led for Enterprise.
9. **Moat thesis:** Vertical-specific workflow lock-in (Skill Library), deep integration into Word, and proprietary legal RAG methods.
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary.
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow (minutes to hours) via chained skills and persistent project-based organization.

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end contract redlining in Word, automated legal research with verifiable citations, and cross-app action execution via connectors.
- **Weaknesses:** High entry price for individuals ($500/mo) may limit bottom-up adoption compared to generalist LLMs.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from a drafting tool to a full "AI workspace" that orchestrates legal operations across the enterprise tech stack.
- **M&A:** none visible
