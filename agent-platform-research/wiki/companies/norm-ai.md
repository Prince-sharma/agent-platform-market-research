## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end conversion of regulations into agents that perform expert reviews of marketing content, negotiate contracts, and complete DDQs.
- **Weaknesses:** High dependency on expert legal engineers to calibrate agents for high-stakes reliability.
- **Trajectory:** Moving toward a full-stack legal model by operating its own AI-native law firm (Norm Law) rather than just selling software.
