# MOVEdot

**Thesis (one line):** AI agent platform for hardware engineering that automates test data analysis and simulation to compress vehicle development cycles.

## Facts

- Founded: 2024
- HQ: San Francisco (or Dover, US)
- YC batch: F25
- Backers: Y Combinator
- Funding: $500K, Seed, 2025-09-29
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: OEMs and tier-1 suppliers in automotive, robotics, and manufacturing
- Pricing model: unknown

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** natural language (Cursor-like interface) and config workflow
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** dedicated VPC (deploy into customer AWS, Azure, or GCP)
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** proprietary connector library (Adams, VI-grade, MATLAB, Canopy)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** live sync with systems of record (files, databases, lakehouses, APIs)
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** approval gates (agents and humans work together; analysis becomes versioned code)
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC, audit trails, SOC 2 Type I, spend caps
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** sales-led (demo-based)
9. **Moat thesis:** workflow lock-in (analysis library) and integrations
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary; self-host option (customer cloud)
11. **Task horizon:** multi-step workflow (minutes)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Agents connect to telemetry and simulation tools to calibrate parameters, analyze failures, and generate reports/dashboards.
- **Weaknesses:** Early stage with limited funding ($500K) relative to the complexity of the physical-world testing market.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from motorsports/race cars into broader manufacturing, robotics, and production lines.
- **M&A:** none visible
