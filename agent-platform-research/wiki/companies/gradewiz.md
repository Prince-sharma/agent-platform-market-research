# GradeWiz

**Thesis (one line):** An AI grading agent for universities that automates student assessment and feedback to reduce instructor workload and accelerate learning cycles.

## Facts

- Founded: 2025 (implied by YC W25 and Cornell Chronicle date)
- HQ: unknown
- YC batch: W25
- Backers: Y Combinator
- Funding: unknown
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: University professors and educators
- Pricing model: unknown (offers 1-month free trials)

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Config workflow (instructing the model on how to grade/rubric generation)
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** unknown
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Proprietary "Symphony Model" and computer vision for segmenting student work
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Approval gates (instructor review required before grades/feedback are released to students)
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** Accessibility compliance (VPAT report, WCAG 2.2)
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led/Pilot-led (pilots at Cornell, Penn State, Syracuse, etc.)
9. **Moat thesis:** Proprietary vision technology (99% accuracy) and specialized grading model
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow (upload, segment, grade, review, release)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end grading automation including computer vision for document segmentation, rubric generation, individualized feedback creation, and class-wide performance analytics.
- **Weaknesses:** High dependency on instructor review for final validation; potential friction in digitizing physical paper submissions.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from early Ivy League and state university pilots to broader institutional adoption; moving toward a "blazing fast TA" positioning.
- **M&A:** None visible.
