# Validio

**Thesis (one line):** Agentic data management platform automating observability and quality to ensure enterprise data is reliable for AI and analytics.

## Facts

- Founded: 2019
- HQ: Stockholm
- YC batch: none
- Backers: Plural (lead), Lakestar, J12 Ventures, Kevin Ryan, Denise Persson, Emil Eifrem
- Funding: $47M total, $30M Series A (2026-03-05)
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: ARR increased 800% in last 12 months (as of 2026-03-05)
- ICP: Data and AI-driven enterprises (Banking, Manufacturing, Telco, Software)
- Pricing model: Custom based on size and complexity of data

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Dashboards and natural language interface for generating SQL and recommending validators.
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Multi-tenant SaaS, managed solution (GCP, AWS, Azure), or secure VPC private cloud hosting.
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary integrations with modern data stack (warehouses, streams, lakes, query engines, BI tools).
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Live sync with systems of record; uses LLMs to profile, classify, and describe structured/unstructured data.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Automated anomaly detection and monitoring setup with smart alerts for human resolution.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, and HIPAA compliance; data encryption in transit and at rest.
7. **Pricing model:** Custom pricing based on number of data assets, segments, and deployment model.
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led enterprise motion with a 14-day free trial.
9. **Moat thesis:** Workflow lock-in through end-to-end automation of the data quality flow from profiling to root-cause analysis.
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary; offers self-hosted VPC option.
11. **Task horizon:** Persistent (always-on monitoring and validation).

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Agents automate the full data quality lifecycle: profiling, classifying data, generating SQL for monitors, and performing root-cause analysis via lineage.
- **Weaknesses:** Pricing is opaque and custom, which may hinder adoption for smaller enterprises compared to seat-based models.
- **Trajectory:** Rapidly scaling GTM in US, UK, and Northern Europe to capitalize on the demand for AI-ready data.
- **M&A:** none visible
