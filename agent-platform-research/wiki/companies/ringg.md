# Ringg

**Thesis (one line):** An enterprise conversational AI platform orchestrating voice, chat, and browser agents to complete complex end-to-end business workflows.

## Facts

- Founded: 2023
- HQ: Bengaluru
- YC batch: none
- Backers: Peak XV (lead), Arkam Ventures, Capital 2B, Groww Founder Fund, Kunal Shah, Whitecap Ventures
- Funding: $15M Series A (August 2026)
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Enterprises in BFSI, Healthcare, E-commerce, and Ed-tech
- Pricing model: Usage-based (per connected minute, per session, or per minute for browser agents)

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** self-serve, no-code
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** vendor multi-tenant SaaS with on-prem deployment option
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** proprietary connectors for CRMs, support systems, and payment tools
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** live sync with systems of record; proprietary STT (Parrot) and fine-tuned LLMs
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** fully autonomous with QA evals for hallucinations and obedience
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** private by default (local inference/processing), auditability, and governance
7. **Pricing model:** platform fee plus usage (per minute/session)
8. **GTM motion:** PLG self-serve and sales-led
9. **Moat thesis:** proprietary speech models (STT/TTS) and cross-channel context preservation
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary; self-host option available
11. **Task horizon:** multi-step workflow (minutes)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Agents handle lead qualification, appointment booking, abandoned cart recovery, and KYC onboarding across voice, WhatsApp, and web browsers.
- **Weaknesses:** unknown
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from a voice-first orchestrator to a cross-modal agent platform including browser-use agents.
- **M&A:** none visible
