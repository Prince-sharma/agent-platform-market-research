# Harvey

**Thesis (one line):** Agentic AI legal associate providing end-to-end legal workflows for elite law firms and corporate legal departments.

## Facts

- Founded: unknown
- HQ: unknown
- YC batch: none
- Backers: Sequoia, Conviction, Coatue, GIC
- Funding: $200M last round (Mar 2026)
- Valuation: $11B (Mar 2026); reportedly seeking $15.5B (Aug 2026)
- Revenue/ARR: ~$100M (2025/2026, estimated)
- ICP: Am Law 100 firms, mid-market law firms, and large corporate legal departments
- Pricing model: Per seat; $100–$200/month (large scale) to $1,000–$2,000/month (mid-market)

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Natural language descriptions for agents; pre-built and custom workflow builders.
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS with enterprise security controls (SAML SSO, IP allow-listing).
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary ecosystem integrating with Word, Outlook, iManage, NetDocuments, SharePoint, and APIs.
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Built-in RAG via "Vault" for bulk analysis; live sync with LexisNexis, Rettsdata, and internal systems.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Agentic planning and execution delivering "review-ready" results for human judgment/approval.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC, audit logs, SOC2 II, CCPA, ISO 27001, and ISO 27701 compliance.
7. **Pricing model:** Per seat with volume discounts; annual contracts typically $50k–$300k+ with seat minimums (25–50).
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led enterprise motion (discovery, pilot, custom quote).
9. **Moat thesis:** Deep vertical integration, data gravity via Vault, and distribution within elite law firms.
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary.
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflows (research, drafting, reviewing) to long-horizon agentic tasks.

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Handles end-to-end legal lifecycles including complex research, document review, predictive analysis, and drafting with full citations.
- **Weaknesses:** Opaque, high-cost pricing structure that may alienate smaller firms; heavy reliance on high-touch sales.
- **Trajectory:** Moving toward a "mega-round" valuation ($15.5B) as it scales from a tool to a comprehensive legal operating system.
- **M&A:** None visible.
