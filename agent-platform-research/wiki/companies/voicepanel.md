# Voicepanel

**Thesis (one line):** An AI agent platform that automates end-to-end customer research, replacing slow traditional methods with scalable, AI-driven user conversations.

## Facts

- Founded: 2023
- HQ: San Francisco
- YC batch: W24
- Backers: Y Combinator, Rebel Fund, Liquid 2 Ventures, TwentyTwo VC, Arash Ferdowsi
- Funding: $2.4M, Seed, May 06, 2024
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Product teams lacking dedicated research resources
- Pricing model: unknown

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** natural language (learning objectives prompts)
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** MCP
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** unknown
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** fully autonomous (recruiting, screening, conducting, and reporting)
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** spend caps, branding, governance, and AI prompt configuration
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** PLG self-serve (free account sign-up)
9. **Moat thesis:** unknown
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** multi-step workflow (minutes to hours)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Automates the entire research lifecycle: recruiting respondents, screening for quality, conducting voice/video/text interviews, and synthesizing results into Slack reports.
- **Weaknesses:** Currently limited to web app product embeds (native mobile apps pending).
- **Trajectory:** Moving toward a comprehensive "feedback engine" integrating diverse stimuli (prototypes, apps) and automated translation for global scale.
- **M&A:** none visible
