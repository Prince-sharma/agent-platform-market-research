# Automat

**Thesis (one line):** A managed agentic automation platform replacing legacy RPA with self-healing AI agents that automate enterprise back-office processes via UI and APIs.

## Facts

- Founded: 2023
- HQ: unknown
- YC batch: W23
- Backers: Felicis (lead), Initialized, Khosla Ventures, Y Combinator, K5 Global, Input Capital
- Funding: $19.25M total; $15.5M Series A
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Enterprise back-office teams, Centers of Excellence (COE), and RPA teams
- Pricing model: Subscription and usage-based

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Natural language and demonstration (screen recording/video context and spoken explanations)
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS, dedicated VPC, and on-premise
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary engine supporting UI-based agents (Windows, Citrix, web), API-driven workflows, and IDP
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Live sync with systems of record via UI/API; uses VLMs and LLMs for context
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Fully autonomous with self-healing/retries and human-in-the-loop feedback for precision
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC, audit trails, versioning, SOC 2, GDPR, HIPAA, ISO 27001
7. **Pricing model:** Platform subscription plus usage-based pricing
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led with forward-deployed engineers for enterprise onboarding
9. **Moat thesis:** Workflow lock-in via managed "Agentic Operating Procedures" (AOP) and rapid deployment speed
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary; offers self-host/on-premise options
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow (minutes) to persistent (always-on) agents

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Agents perform end-to-end back-office tasks by reasoning through UI interactions, extracting data from documents (IDP), and calling APIs, with the ability to self-heal when UI elements change.
- **Weaknesses:** Heavy reliance on forward-deployed engineers for initial enterprise setup suggests a high-touch onboarding process.
- **Trajectory:** Moving toward a complete replacement of the legacy RPA market by lowering the total cost of ownership and maintenance.
- **M&A:** none visible
