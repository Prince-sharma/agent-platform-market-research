## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Unified extensible platform combining AI agents, intelligent assistants, and partner tools
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Flexible deployment; supports full platform, composable agents, and hyperscaler ecosystems
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Marketplace of Wipro-custom and 15+ third-party partner agents
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Built-in RAG and fine-tuning capabilities via Snowflake Cortex AI integration
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Supports autonomous systems with HITL, governance, and audit logging (e.g., ZeroClawAI)
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** Built-in guardrails, policy enforcement, and security layers via NVIDIA NemoClaw
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** Services-led; integrated into Wipro Intelligence offerings
9. **Moat thesis:** Distribution through SI relationships and deep domain-specific agent libraries
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary platform leveraging NVIDIA AI Enterprise stack and OpenClaw
11. **Task horizon:** Long-horizon; covers end-to-end SDLC from requirements to production-ready software

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Automates full SDLC (design, build, test, release), regulatory compliance auditing for banks, and proactive code drift detection.
- **Weaknesses:** Heavy reliance on partner stacks (NVIDIA, Snowflake) for core infrastructure and security.
- **Trajectory:** Moving from AI-assisted services to self-optimizing, agent-native delivery ecosystems.
- **M&A:** none visible
