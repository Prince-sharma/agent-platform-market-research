# TrainLoop

**Thesis (one line):** RL fine-tuning platform that enables enterprises to build specialized reasoning models for complex, long-horizon business tasks.

- Founded: 2025
- HQ: San Francisco, CA
- YC batch: W25

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** SDK and platform for curating training signals and deploying tuned models.
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** unknown
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** unknown
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Curates training signals from real usage to train reward models.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Works with human experts to train their intuition into proprietary models.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** unknown
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** unknown
9. **Moat thesis:** Proprietary reward models and specialized post-training strategies for long-horizon tasks.
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** long-horizon (hours to weeks)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Enables the creation of domain-specific reasoning systems capable of handling complex vertical tasks like codegen, compliance, and healthcare.
- **Weaknesses:** Early stage with no visible public traction or pricing transparency.
- **Trajectory:** Moving toward becoming a specialized post-training lab for high-stakes enterprise reasoning.
