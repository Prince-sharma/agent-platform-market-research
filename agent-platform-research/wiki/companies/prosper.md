# Prosper

**Thesis (one line):** AI voice agents automating the end-to-end patient journey to reduce healthcare administrative waste and improve access to care.

## Facts

- Founded: 2023
- HQ: New York, NY
- YC batch: YC S23
- Backers: Andreessen Horowitz (lead), Base10, Emergence Capital, Y Combinator, Company Ventures
- Funding: $35M total ($30M Series A, June 2026; $5M Seed)
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown (reported 5x revenue growth as of June 2026)
- ICP: Healthcare organizations, including outpatient groups and health systems
- Pricing model: unknown

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** No-code onboarding
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Cloud or on-premise
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary connector library (80+ integrations including Athenahealth, Kareo, AdvancedMD)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Live sync with systems of record (EHR)
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Fully autonomous with internal testing and iteration phases
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA compliant, full QA visibility and reporting
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led
9. **Moat thesis:** Workflow lock-in via EHR integrations and end-to-end patient journey coverage
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary; self-host option available
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow (minutes)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Handles patient and payor calls for scheduling, intake, insurance benefit verification (including calling insurers directly to navigate IVR), and patient billing.
- **Weaknesses:** High dependency on EHR API stability and payor IVR accessibility.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from simple scheduling to a comprehensive "patient journey" platform covering all financial and administrative touchpoints.
- **M&A:** none visible
