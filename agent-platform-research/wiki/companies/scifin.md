# SciFin

**Thesis (one line):** AI agents that reconcile fragmented revenue data into a "system of reality" to close the context gap for GTM teams.

## Facts

- Founded: 2024
- HQ: San Francisco, California
- YC batch: none
- Backers: Altimeter (lead), Madrona (lead), Foundation Capital, S32, Zetta Ventures
- Funding: $44M seed, 2026-09-01
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Midmarket and enterprise revenue organizations (Sales leaders, RevOps, Finance)
- Pricing model: unknown

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Natural language chatbot (web-like interface similar to ChatGPT), voice, email, Slack, and WhatsApp.
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** unknown
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Connects across finance, accounts, deals, forecasts, reps, territories, and customer conversations.
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Maintains a curated context of business knowledge; continually reconciles scattered system data with reality.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Specialist AI agents track metrics and update reports; Pixie provides recommended actions for human execution.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** unknown
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** unknown
9. **Moat thesis:** Trusted context and "context convergence" that reduces reliance on fragmented systems of record.
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** Persistent (always-on agents that update knowledge after every meeting and track day-to-day operations).

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Agents perform continuous data reconciliation across GTM stacks to provide real-time answers, reports, and coaching priorities.
- **Weaknesses:** Currently focused narrowly on revenue/GTM; requires deep integration into multiple fragmented enterprise systems to be effective.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from GTM into broader finance and quarterly planning use cases to further consolidate the enterprise tool stack.
- **M&A:** none visible
