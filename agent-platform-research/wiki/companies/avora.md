# Avora

**Thesis (one line):** AI agents for dental operations that automate patient education, treatment plan conversion, and clinical charting for DSOs.

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Agents listen to appointments to generate chart notes, create interactive personalized treatment plans for patient education, and execute follow-up messaging to drive re-care and case acceptance.
- **Weaknesses:** High dependency on integration with fragmented dental practice management software.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from a scribe/education tool into a full operational layer for patient conversion and retention.
