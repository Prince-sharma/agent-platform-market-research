# Klarity

**Thesis (one line):** Enterprise AI platform automating document-heavy accounting and compliance workflows to replace manual review with structured financial data.

## Facts

- Founded: 2017
- HQ: San Francisco, California
- YC batch: S18
- Backers: Nat Friedman and Daniel Gross (lead), Tola Capital, Scale Venture Partners, Picus Capital, Invus Capital, Y Combinator
- Funding: $90M total, $70M Series B (2024-06-24)
- Valuation: unknown (Jan 2022 estimate $72.53M - $90.53M)
- Revenue/ARR: $7M (2026-06-23, low confidence)
- ICP: CFOs, controllers, and finance professionals at enterprise SaaS and professional services companies
- Pricing model: Custom enterprise pricing scaled to document volume, modules, and users

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** unknown
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** proprietary connector library (integrates with NetSuite, SAP, Oracle)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** built-in RAG for contract and internal record processing
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** approval gates (replaces manual work with a review task)
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** audit trails (produces auditor-ready output for SOX testing)
7. **Pricing model:** platform fee plus usage (scaled to volume)
8. **GTM motion:** sales-led
9. **Moat thesis:** workflow lock-in through deep integration with ERPs and compliance standards (ASC 606, IFRS 15, ASC 842)
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** multi-step workflow (minutes)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end automation of revenue recognition, extracting performance obligations and calculating schedules from contracts to feed ERPs.
- **Weaknesses:** High dependency on complex enterprise sales cycles and specific accounting standards.
- **Trajectory:** Repositioned in 2026 as "Within", a Company Brain that maps how people work so agents can work alongside them (DoorDash, ServiceNow case studies); expanding from contract review into process intelligence and work reallocation.
- **M&A:** none visible
