# Eve

**Thesis (one line):** AI workforce automating end-to-end casework and operations for plaintiff-side law firms to increase case value and efficiency.

## Facts

- Founded: unknown
- HQ: unknown
- YC batch: none
- Backers: Menlo Ventures (seed)
- Funding: $103M (Series B, 2025-09-30)
- Valuation: $1B (2025-09-30)
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Plaintiff law firms (specifically personal injury and labor/employment)
- Pricing model: Demo-gated / custom quotes

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Natural language (Analyst) and proactive agentic workflows (EveOS); or unknown
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS; or unknown
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary connectors for CMS/DMS (Clio Grow, Smart Advocate, Litify) and custom MCPs for private databases.
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Live sync with systems of record via Eve Atlas; built-in RAG for case law and medical literature.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Permissioned agents acting through approved workflows with human review of outputs and decisions.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** unknown
7. **Pricing model:** Custom quotes via sales demo; no public per-seat or per-run pricing.
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led (demo-gated).
9. **Moat thesis:** Vertical-specific data gravity (Atlas), deep integration into legal CMS, and specialized legal/medical RAG.
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary; provides open APIs for custom systems.
11. **Task horizon:** Persistent (always-on) for intake and overnight auditing; multi-step workflows for drafting and research.

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Full-lifecycle automation including 24/7 multilingual intake, automated document drafting (demands, complaints), overnight case auditing, and live firm analytics.
- **Weaknesses:** Opaque pricing model creates friction for adoption; highly narrow vertical focus limits total addressable market.
- **Trajectory:** Moving from a set of AI tools to a full "AI-Native" operating system (EveOS) that replaces manual case management.
- **M&A:** none visible
