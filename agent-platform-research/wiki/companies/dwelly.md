# Dwelly

**Thesis (one line):** An AI-native operating system that automates UK lettings agencies by acquiring firms and migrating them to an AI-driven service layer.

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Agents automate end-to-end operations including tenant/landlord communication, maintenance coordination, rent collection, and compliance.
- **Weaknesses:** Reliance on self-reported productivity figures (e.g., 300 units per agent) without independent audit.
- **Trajectory:** Scaling via an "AI-forward acquisition plan" to turn traditional service agencies into software-driven entities.
- **M&A:** Actively acquiring independent UK lettings agencies to migrate them onto the Dwelly operating system.
