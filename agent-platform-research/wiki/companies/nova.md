# Nova

**Thesis (one line):** An agentic AI platform for SAP that automates the modernization, generation, and maintenance of critical enterprise software and custom code.

## Facts

- Founded: unknown
- HQ: San Francisco
- YC batch: none
- Backers: Chemistry (lead), Accel, Conviction, SAP.io
- Funding: >$40M total; $31.5M Series A (2026-05-05)
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Fortune 500 companies using SAP (specifically those migrating to S/4HANA)
- Pricing model: Pilot engagement ($50,000 one-time); other pricing upon request

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Natural language and guided workflows for code analysis, documentation, and development.
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** unknown
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Integrates with SAP landscapes (SAP HANA, SAP ERP, BTP) and customer toolchains.
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Live sync/analysis of the customer's entire SAP landscape, including custom code, configurations, and processes.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Autonomous end-to-end execution (planning, executing, self-correcting) with human review, decisions, or approval gates.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** Enterprise-grade controls for shipping code changes.
7. **Pricing model:** Platform fee (Pilot) and custom pricing upon request.
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led targeting flagship enterprise logos.
9. **Moat thesis:** Deep vertical specialization in SAP's proprietary language and the $89B S/4HANA migration wave.
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary.
11. **Task horizon:** Long-horizon (capable of compressing multi-month programs into a single day).

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Agents perform end-to-end SAP lifecycle tasks: analyzing legacy custom code, generating "as-is/to-be" documentation, identifying "Clean Core" alternatives, writing new ABAP/BTP code, and triaging production tickets.
- **Weaknesses:** High dependency on the SAP ecosystem and the specific timeline of the S/4HANA migration deadline.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from a migration-focused "wedge" into a permanent system of action for daily SAP maintenance and eventually extending to other enterprise systems.
- **M&A:** none visible
