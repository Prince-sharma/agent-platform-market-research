# Atomicwork

**Thesis (one line):** An agentic AI platform for IT and employee service management that automates routine IT workflows to replace legacy ITSM systems.

## Facts

- Founded: 2022
- HQ: San Francisco
- YC batch: none
- Backers: Khosla Ventures (lead), Z47 (lead), Blume Ventures, Battery Ventures, Peak XV Partners, Neon Fund, Storm Ventures
- Funding: $39M total ($25M Series A, January 28, 2025; $14M seed)
- Valuation: $150M (January 28, 2025)
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Mid-market companies and global organizations; specifically IT and enterprise service teams.
- Pricing model: Usage-based (credits) and outcome-based plans.

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Natural language and config workflow (via "AI Coworkers" and custom roles/skills).
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS (with custom data residency for Enterprise).
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary connector library (integrations with ServiceNow, Atlassian JSM, Microsoft Teams, Slack, and Lansweeper).
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Built-in enterprise knowledge graph for real-time context across users, assets, and support history.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Fully autonomous capabilities (reason, plan, and execute) with multi-agent orchestration.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC, enterprise security/compliance, and custom data residency.
7. **Pricing model:** Platform fee plus usage (credits based on steps, tools, and reasoning complexity) and per-agent fees ($499/worker/month for additional coworkers).
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led (demo-driven) with a "Bring Your Own ITSM" entry point to lower migration friction.
9. **Moat thesis:** Workflow lock-in and data gravity via the enterprise knowledge graph and deep ITSM/ESM integrations.
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary.
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow (capable of handling major incidents and self-healing systems).

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Agents act as "AI Coworkers" (e.g., ITOps Support Engineer) that can autonomously handle ticket deflection, self-service, and execute end-to-end service delivery workflows.
- **Weaknesses:** Squeezed between massive suites (ServiceNow) and highly funded independents (Resolve); relies on "Bring Your Own ITSM" to compete with incumbents.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from pure IT service management (ITSM) into broader Enterprise Service Management (ESM) and automating more complex business use cases.
- **M&A:** None visible.
