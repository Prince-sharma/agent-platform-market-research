# Robin AI

**Thesis (one line):** AI contract copilot automating legal drafting, review, and intelligence for enterprise legal teams to scale operations.

## Facts

- Founded: unknown
- HQ: unknown
- YC batch: none
- Backers: Plural
- Funding: $51M total; £31.2M rescue round (2026-03-23)
- Valuation: £163M (2026-03-23)
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: In-house legal teams at large enterprises (e.g., Pfizer, KPMG, UBS)
- Pricing model: Custom annual contracts; typically $50K-$250K/yr

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Natural language and Microsoft Word integration for drafting; web app for analysis.
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS.
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary connectors for CLM and DMS.
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Built-in RAG with pinpoint citations and live sync of legal precedents.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Approval gates; legal team escalation for high-risk flags.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** SOC 2 Type 2, RBAC, and audit trails.
7. **Pricing model:** Custom annual platform fee; tiered by Reports, Platform, and Copilot.
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led enterprise motion.
9. **Moat thesis:** Workflow lock-in via precedent libraries and deep integration into legal drafting (Word).
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary.
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow (minutes to hours) for contract review and negotiation.

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end contract lifecycle management including drafting, redlining, risk flagging, and structured metadata extraction from document corpora.
- **Weaknesses:** User reports indicate technical limitations and a persistent need for human oversight to correct AI errors.
- **Trajectory:** Shifting from a simple copilot to a "Legal Intelligence Platform" focusing on corpus-wide data insights and obligation management.
- **M&A:** None visible.
