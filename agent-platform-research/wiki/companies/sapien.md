# Sapien

**Thesis (one line):** An AI operational analysis platform for enterprise finance that automates complex financial workflows and connects operational decisions to P&L outcomes.

## Facts

- Founded: 2024
- HQ: San Francisco
- YC batch: none
- Backers: General Catalyst (lead), Neo, Bryan Baum (K5), Russell Kaplen, Claire Hughes Johnson, Sabrina Hahn, Scott Belsky
- Funding: $8.7M seed, October 2024
- Valuation: $180M (September 2026)
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Enterprise finance and operations teams (e.g., large industrials, manufacturing, retail, private equity)
- Pricing model: unknown

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** natural language
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** proprietary connector library (connects to ERPs, warehouses, spreadsheets)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** live sync with systems of record (governed company understanding of definitions and data relationships)
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** autonomous coworkers; reviewed definitions
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC, audit trails, access rules
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** sales-led
9. **Moat thesis:** data gravity (governed company understanding) and workflow lock-in
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** multi-step workflow (minutes)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Automates P&L and margin variance decomposition, flags financial risk/margin erosion, and handles annual planning logic roll-forwards.
- **Weaknesses:** Potential for attribution errors and the resulting need for rigorous audit trails.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from specialized FP&A software into broader operational analysis for large industrial enterprises.
- **M&A:** none visible
