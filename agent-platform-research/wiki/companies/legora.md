# Legora

**Thesis (one line):** An agentic operating system for law firms that automates complex legal workflows from research to last-mile delivery.

## Facts

- Founded: unknown
- HQ: Sweden
- YC batch: none
- Backers: General Catalyst, Menlo, Bessemer, Atlassian, nVentures
- Funding: $680M+ total; $600M Series D (Apr 2026)
- Valuation: $5.55B (Mar 2026); seeking $10B+
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Law firms (global, mid-sized), in-house legal teams, public sector
- Pricing model: Consumption-based for Agent Pro; seat-based for core platform

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Multi-modal: aOS, Word Add-In, Outlook Add-In, Editor, Mobile app, and Portal.
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS.
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary "Agentic Harness" connecting data and integrations.
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Built-in RAG/knowledge layer; zero AI training on customer data.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Agent plans, executes, reviews, and delivers; lawyers provide final judgment and decisions.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC, SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, ISO 42001, GDPR, HIPAA, and spending controls.
7. **Pricing model:** Consumption-based pricing (CBP) for Agent Pro; attributed to specific projects/matters.
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led (enterprise licenses, campus-wide licenses).
9. **Moat thesis:** Vertical-specific workflow lock-in (aOS), deep legal capabilities, and high-compliance certifications.
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary.
11. **Task horizon:** Long-horizon; end-to-end execution of complex legal work.

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Agents perform end-to-end legal work including research, planning, execution, and review across M&A, Litigation, Banking, and Tax.
- **Weaknesses:** High valuation pressure ($10B+ target) may necessitate aggressive pricing shifts.
- **Trajectory:** Moving from a SaaS tool to an "agentic operating system" (aOS) that replaces billable hours with outcome-based value.
- **M&A:** none visible
