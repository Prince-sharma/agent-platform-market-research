# Factory

**Thesis (one line):** An agentic software-development platform using autonomous Droids to triage, code, validate, and ship software across the entire SDLC.

## Facts

- Founded: unknown
- HQ: San Francisco
- YC batch: none
- Backers: NEA (lead), Sequoia Capital, NVIDIA, J.P. Morgan, Abstract Ventures, Mantis Ventures
- Funding: $50M Series B (2025-09-25)
- Valuation: $300M (2025-09-25)
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Individual developers and large engineering teams automating real delivery work
- Pricing model: Tiered monthly subscription (Pro, Plus, Max) and custom Business plans

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Multi-platform: Desktop app, CLI, SDK, browser, Slack, and IDEs (VS Code, JetBrains, Vim)
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Cloud and local background agents; Factory-managed cloud computers for remote Droids
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Custom integrations for organization workflows; model-independent routing (Claude, GPT, Gemini)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Agentic search for full codebase understanding across entire repositories without manual context selection
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Adjustable autonomy from fully supervised to autonomous execution; user-controlled boundaries for edits and approvals
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** SSO, SAML/SCIM provisioning, Zero Data Retention (ZDR), audit logging, and activity trails
7. **Pricing model:** Per seat monthly: Pro ($20), Plus ($100), Max ($200); Business is custom
8. **GTM motion:** PLG self-serve for individuals; sales-led for Business/Enterprise
9. **Moat thesis:** Enterprise abstraction for codebase modernization and high performance on Terminal Bench
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary; offers "Droid Core" as a free open-weight model pool
11. **Task horizon:** Long-horizon; autonomous agents that triage, write, test, and ship code from "one prompt to PR"

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Droids operate across the full SDLC, performing tasks such as triaging issues, writing and validating code, and shipping releases. They can make coordinated changes across hundreds of files and handle legacy code modernization.
- **Weaknesses:** unknown
- **Trajectory:** Moving toward becoming a general agent platform that enables enterprise-scale codebase modernization and automated migration.
- **M&A:** none visible
