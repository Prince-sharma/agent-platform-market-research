# Minimal AI

**Thesis (one line):** AI support teams for e-commerce brands that resolve most tickets autonomously, managed through a single conversational AI manager.

## Facts

- Founded: 2024
- HQ: Amsterdam, Netherlands
- YC batch: S25
- Backers: unknown beyond YC
- Funding: unknown
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: E-commerce brands (Shopify-centric stacks)
- Pricing model: unknown

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Natural language via the "AI Manager" conversational interface; transparent agent reasoning with instant protocol updates
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** unknown
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Integrates fragmented e-commerce stacks (Shopify, Zendesk, ShipBob, WMS, and similar tools)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Protocol-based knowledge trained via user feedback; AI Manager spots gaps and proactively suggests improvements
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Agents resolve tickets autonomously; humans supervise and correct through the AI Manager
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** unknown
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** PLG/demo-led (book a demo with founders)
9. **Moat thesis:** Claims tens of thousands of tickets resolved daily; per-brand protocol knowledge compounding
10. **Open-source posture:** unknown
11. **Task horizon:** Persistent (always-on support team across channels)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Resolves e-commerce tickets end-to-end (census: 80% of interactions; launch materials cite 80-90% at human-level quality), including policy-heavy cases like refunds and shipping.
- **Weaknesses:** No pricing, funding, or governance details public; three-person team in a crowded e-commerce support-agent market.
- **Trajectory:** YC S25; claimed volume of tens of thousands of tickets per day as of the August 2025 launch.
- **M&A:** none visible
