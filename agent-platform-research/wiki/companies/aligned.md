# Aligned

**Thesis (one line):** An AI-native deal workspace providing seller and buyer agents to streamline B2B sales execution and collaboration.

## Facts

- Founded: unknown
- HQ: unknown
- YC batch: none
- Backers: unknown

- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown (ARR tripled pre-Series B raise)
- ICP: B2B sales teams, SMB and Mid-Market
- Pricing model: Per seat; Starter ($0), Basic ($29-$35), Pro ($60)

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Drag-and-drop Room editor for layout and content personalization.
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS.
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** MCP (Claude, ChatGPT), Gong, PandaDoc, and bi-directional CRM integrations (Salesforce, Hubspot).
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Live sync with systems of record (CRM, call transcripts, email, calendar) for AI insights.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Approval gates via Mutual Action Plans; AI agents (Client Assist) handle buyer interactions based on playbooks.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC (secured tabs), SSO, audit trails (room analytics), and guaranteed SLAs for Enterprise.
7. **Pricing model:** Per seat; tiered monthly/annual billing with Enterprise custom pricing.
8. **GTM motion:** PLG self-serve (Free/Basic plans) and sales-led for Enterprise.
9. **Moat thesis:** Workflow lock-in via Mutual Action Plans and data gravity from cross-source deal context.
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary.
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow (minutes to days) managing the end-to-end deal lifecycle.

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Deploys "Buyer Copilots" to answer prospect questions via playbooks and "Seller Copilots" for deal summaries and forecasting.
- **Weaknesses:** High dependency on CRM data quality for AI highlights and insights to be accurate.
- **Trajectory:** Moving toward becoming the "System of Action" for B2B sales, absorbing CPQ-like configuration functions.
- **M&A:** None visible.
