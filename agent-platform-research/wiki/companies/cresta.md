# Cresta

**Thesis (one line):** Contact-center AI combining autonomous agents with real-time human agent assistance, built on conversation intelligence from Stanford AI Lab roots.

## Facts

- Founded: 2017 (Zayd Enam, Tim Shi, Sebastian Thrun; Stanford AI Lab)
- HQ: Palo Alto, CA
- YC batch: none
- Backers: World Innovation Lab and QIA (led $125M Series D, Nov 2024); Greylock, a16z, Sequoia, Accenture
- Funding: $276M total; $125M Series D (Nov 2024)
- Valuation: $1.6B at Series C (Mar 2022); Series D undisclosed (third-party est. ~$750M); ~$1.1B implied (Aug 2026)
- Revenue/ARR: $100M+ ARR (Apr 2026, Axios Pro)
- ICP: Fortune 500 contact centers in regulated industries (healthcare, banking, insurance, travel)
- Pricing model: Annual subscription tiers plus per-interaction overage (AWS Marketplace: ~$150K/yr for 125K chats or 100K calls; $1.20/chat, $1.50/call over)

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** No-code workflow orchestration (Opera) plus natural-language agent development (Conductor).
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS.
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary connector library for CRM and telephony stacks; MCP support.
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Built-in RAG over enterprise knowledge bases plus real-time conversation context.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Hybrid; autonomous virtual agents plus real-time human agent assistance with supervisory guardrails.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC, SOC 2, ISO 27001/27701/42001, HIPAA, PCI DSS, PII redaction; "AI agent defense" security framework.
7. **Pricing model:** Annual subscription tiers plus per-interaction overage (AWS Marketplace listing).
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led enterprise motion; Accenture is a strategic investor.
9. **Moat thesis:** Data gravity from contact-center conversations, hybrid human+AI positioning, regulated-industry compliance.
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary.
11. **Task horizon:** Single-turn to multi-step (minutes) conversation workflows; always-on agent assistance.

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Autonomous voice and digital agents resolving conversations end-to-end; real-time guidance, coaching, and QA for human agents.
- **Weaknesses:** Impacted by the June 2026 Klue vendor breach (stolen OAuth credentials); Series D reportedly a down round; opaque list pricing.
- **Trajectory:** $100M+ ARR (Apr 2026) after a 2024 reset; expanding from agent assistance into autonomous agents (Conductor, Opera).
- **M&A:** None visible.
