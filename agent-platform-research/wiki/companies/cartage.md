# Cartage

**Thesis (one line):** An autonomous freight coordination system that replaces brokers and logistics teams to reduce shipping costs for manufacturers and distributors.

## Facts

- Founded: unknown
- HQ: San Francisco
- YC batch: S24
- Backers: unknown
- Funding: $3.3M (last round date 2024-10-10)
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: $150k ARR in under 4 months (early stage, reported by YC)
- ICP: Manufacturers and distributors in North America
- Pricing model: unknown

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** natural language (email, Slack, Teams)
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** unknown
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** integrates with ERP, spreadsheets, email, Slack, and Teams
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** reads and writes across customer ERPs and spreadsheets
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** fully autonomous with human-in-the-loop fallback (Cartage team steps in if things go wrong)
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** unknown
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** sales-led
9. **Moat thesis:** workflow lock-in and data gravity (lane intelligence and carrier performance patterns)
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** persistent (always-on operator)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** The agent "Wilson" handles end-to-end freight operations including sourcing carriers, renegotiating rates, confirming PODs, and managing customer communications via email, calls, and Slack.
- **Weaknesses:** Early-stage company pivoting from selling software to brokers to replacing them entirely.
- **Trajectory:** Moving toward a "service-as-software" model that embeds AI agents directly into the logistics workflow to eliminate human coordinators.
- **M&A:** none visible
