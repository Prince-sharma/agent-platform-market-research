## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Model Development Suite via NVIDIA AI Foundry and agent orchestration framework
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Multi-cloud, edge, and on-premises (EY.ai Enterprise Private via Dell/NVIDIA)
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** NVIDIA AI-Q Blueprints and NVIDIA NIM microservices
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Curated domain data combined with NVIDIA reasoning models
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Approval gates via EY Responsible AI (RAI) framework, NVIDIA NeMo Guardrails, and EY SafePrompt
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC and compliance focused; specialized for regulated industries (data sovereignty/stay-on-prem)
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** Services-led; co-sell approach with NVIDIA and Dell Technologies
9. **Moat thesis:** Domain expertise (tax/risk/finance) and deep infrastructure partnerships (NVIDIA/Dell)
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary; offers private/on-prem deployment options
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflows (e.g., automating document review for third-party risk management)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end automation of tax compliance, regulatory reporting, financial crimes detection, and third-party risk management (TPRM) document review.
- **Weaknesses:** Heavy reliance on NVIDIA's stack for core reasoning and orchestration capabilities.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from core finance/tax domains into life sciences and manufacturing; moving toward "physical AI" integration.
- **M&A:** none visible
