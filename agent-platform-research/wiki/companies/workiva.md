# Workiva

**Thesis (one line):** Compliance reporting suite integrating specialized AI agents to automate high-stakes financial, risk, and sustainability disclosures for enterprises.

## Facts

- Founded: 2007
- HQ: Ames, Iowa
- YC batch: none
- Backers: public company (WK)
- Funding: public company (WK)
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Finance, audit, risk, and sustainability teams in regulated enterprises
- Pricing model: unknown

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** natural language (chat, intelligent companion, inline editor)
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** proprietary connector library
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** built-in RAG (Workiva Knowledge persistent intelligence layer)
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** approval gates; traceable and audit-ready outputs
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC, audit trails, BYOK for chat history
7. **Pricing model:** platform fee plus usage (available in advanced solution tiers)
8. **GTM motion:** sales-led
9. **Moat thesis:** data gravity, workflow lock-in, distribution (incumbent suite)
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** multi-step workflow (minutes)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Specialized agents for Tie-Out (variance checking), Benchmarking (SEC peer analysis), and Sustainability (ESRS/ISSB gap assessments).
- **Weaknesses:** High dependency on advanced tier adoption for agentic features.
- **Trajectory:** Moving from execution to orchestration via a new agentic engine and tool-calling architecture.
- **M&A:** none visible
