# Patronus AI

**Thesis (one line):** Infrastructure for agent evaluation and simulation using Digital World Models to stress-test and optimize long-horizon AI agent reliability.

## Facts

- Founded: 2023
- HQ: San Francisco
- YC batch: none
- Backers: Greenfield Partners (lead), Lightspeed Venture Partners, Notable Capital, Datadog, Samsung, Gokul Rajaram, Factorial Capital
- Funding: $50M Series B, 2026-06-25
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: 15x revenue (2026-09-09)
- ICP: AI researchers, agent developers, financial firms, and enterprise teams building agentic systems
- Pricing model: Hybrid (Subscription and pay-as-you-go API)

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Hybrid; provides a centralized evaluation platform (dashboard) and a self-serve API.
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Multi-tenant SaaS (Web App).
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary evaluators (Lynx, Glider) and Digital World Models for simulating digital workflows.
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Provides off-the-shelf adversarial testing sets (e.g., FinanceBench) and supports logging/traces.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Provides guardrails via API to detect and prevent failures (hallucinations, safety risks) in production.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** Includes monitoring and governing of model outputs; RBAC/audit trails unknown.
7. **Pricing model:** Tiered subscription (Free, Base at $25/mo, Enterprise) and pay-as-you-go API.
8. **GTM motion:** PLG self-serve (Free/Base tiers) and sales-led (Enterprise).
9. **Moat thesis:** Research-grade evaluation models (Lynx, Glider) and proprietary Digital World Models for high-alpha simulation.
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary.
11. **Task horizon:** Long-horizon (designed for multi-step complex tasks and long-term agent trajectories).

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Enables developers to simulate agents in "digital worlds" to test multi-step workflows and detect 20+ failure modes via the Percival debugger.
- **Weaknesses:** Pricing structure is noted as confusing due to overlapping Enterprise and Developer tiers.
- **Trajectory:** Shifting from static dataset evaluation (Phase I) to dynamic, real-world simulation and world-model-driven training (Phase II).
- **M&A:** none visible
