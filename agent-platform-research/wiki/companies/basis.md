# Basis

**Thesis (one line):** An AI agent platform automating end-to-end accounting, tax, and audit workflows for professional accounting firms.

## Facts

- Founded: 2023
- HQ: New York
- YC batch: none
- Backers: Accel (lead), GV, Khosla Ventures, Lloyd Blankfein, NFDG, Better Tomorrow Ventures, BoxGroup, Avid Ventures, Kris Fredrickson, Aaron Levie, Adam D’Angelo
- Funding: $100M Series B, February 2026
- Valuation: $1.15B (February 2026)
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Accounting, tax, and audit firms
- Pricing model: Custom quotation based

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** unknown
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** proprietary connector library (integrates with ledgers, file systems, accounting apps, and ERPs)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** live sync with systems of record
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** approval gates (agents deliver finished output for human review; "doers become reviewers")
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC, audit trails (immutable), confidence scoring
7. **Pricing model:** platform fee plus usage (custom pricing)
8. **GTM motion:** sales-led
9. **Moat thesis:** workflow lock-in, domain-specific agent capabilities
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** long-horizon (hours to weeks)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Executes end-to-end complex accounting tasks including month-end closes, partnership tax workbooks (Form 1065), journal entries, and audit testing.
- **Weaknesses:** Moderate setup time (1-3 hours); high reliance on structured accounting data.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from basic bookkeeping into complex tax and audit modules to define the agentic accounting category.
- **M&A:** none visible
