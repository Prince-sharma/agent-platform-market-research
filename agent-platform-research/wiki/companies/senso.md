# Senso

**Thesis (one line):** Infrastructure for the agentic web that allows enterprises to control and verify how AI agents represent and reason over their brand data.

## Facts

- Founded: 2018
- HQ: Toronto, Ontario, Canada
- YC batch: W24
- Backers: unknown
- Funding: $5.6M total; $3M Pre-Series A (per census)
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: $1M annual revenue (2026-06-20)
- ICP: Marketing and support teams, specifically in financial services and credit unions
- Pricing model: unknown

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Dashboards for tracking prompts, evaluations, and remediation workflows
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** unknown
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** unknown
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Built-in knowledge base for publishing verified, structured ground truth
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Human-in-the-loop via review and publish workflow for remediation drafts
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** Verified context and approved enterprise ground truth
7. **Pricing model:** unknown

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Enables agents to safely answer, recommend, and transact using verified first-party enterprise data.
- **Weaknesses:** Initial heavy concentration in the financial services vertical.
