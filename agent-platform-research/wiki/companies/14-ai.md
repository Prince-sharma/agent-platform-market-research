# 14.ai

**Thesis (one line):** AI-native customer service agency that replaces a startup's entire support operation, agents plus human AI engineers, as a single line item.

## Facts

- Founded: unknown (YC W24); founders Marie Schneegans and Michael Fester, a married duo who met in Paris (previously GloGlo.com; Fester co-founded Snips, acquired by Sonos)
- HQ: San Francisco (AI engineers SF-based)
- YC batch: W24
- Backers: Y Combinator (lead per TechCrunch), General Catalyst, Base Case Capital, SV Angel, plus founders of Dropbox, Slack, Replit, and Vercel
- Funding: $3M seed (announced 2026-03-02)
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: B2C startups in e-commerce, mobile apps, fintech, healthcare (customers incl. Brilliant, Sperm Worms, Creative Lighting, Yon-Ka)
- Pricing model: unknown; 7-day free trial; sold as one line item replacing total support spend

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** None for customers; 14.ai's own AI engineers configure and improve the agents (agency model).
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Unknown (managed service; no self-host visible).
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Monitors email, calls, chat, TikTok, Facebook, Telegram, and WhatsApp (Twilio voice); "agentic resolution" plugs into store backends to verify purchases, generate shipping labels, and trigger refunds; integrates with an existing support system within a day.
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Learns each customer's policies; human edge-case interventions are fed back into the agents.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Agents resolve autonomously 24/7; full-time human AI engineers handle edge cases and continuously improve the agents.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** SOC 2 (reported); details unknown.
7. **Pricing model:** Unknown; 7-day free trial.
8. **GTM motion:** Services-led agency/BPO replacement; YC launch plus founder-story PR (TechCrunch).
9. **Moat thesis:** AI-native agency economics: replaces ticketing tools, BPO labor, and management overhead in one line item; compounding edge-case data.
10. **Open-source posture:** Unknown; nothing visible (appears proprietary).
11. **Task horizon:** Always-on persistent (24/7 service).

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Agents clear ticket backlogs across channels with end-to-end actions (verify purchase, generate label, trigger refund); human engineers close edge cases; claims every onboarded customer repurposed their support team before the trial ended.
- **Weaknesses:** $3M seed and small team; services-heavy model constrains margins and scale; no public pricing; early, brand-light customer list.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from support into sales and revenue-growth workflows (TechCrunch, 2026-03).
- **M&A:** none visible.
