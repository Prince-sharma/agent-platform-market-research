## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** config workflow and templates
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** proprietary connector library
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** unknown
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** fully autonomous with pre-built templates
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** sub-accounts access and spend caps via credits
7. **Pricing model:** platform fee plus usage (STT, LLM, TTS, and telephony)
8. **GTM motion:** PLG self-serve and sales-led
9. **Moat thesis:** regional language specialization (40+ languages) and low-latency orchestration
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** multi-step workflow (minutes)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end voice automation including lead qualification, customer support, appointment booking, and specialized HR functions like CV screening and situational roleplay.
- **Weaknesses:** High dependency on third-party model providers for core intelligence (STT/LLM/TTS).
- **Trajectory:** Expanding deep tech stack to support "India's next billion" through native accent and language optimization.
- **M&A:** none visible
