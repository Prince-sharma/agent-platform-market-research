# MinusX

**Thesis (one line):** An agentic data platform that operates existing BI tools like Metabase and Jupyter to automate quantitative research and analysis.

## Facts

- Founded: 2024
- HQ: San Francisco
- YC batch: S24
- Backers: Y Combinator
- Funding: $500K, Seed, 2024
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Founders, data teams, and non-technical business users
- Pricing model: unknown

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** natural language
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** vendor multi-tenant SaaS, self-host
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** MCP, Chrome extension (operates existing apps via clicking/typing)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** live sync with systems of record (Postgres, BigQuery, CSVs, Google Sheets)
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** fully autonomous (operates apps); user can guide/correct in chat
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** unknown
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** PLG self-serve (Chrome extension)
9. **Moat thesis:** evals, workflow lock-in (integrates into existing tools)
10. **Open-source posture:** OSS core; self-host option
11. **Task horizon:** multi-step workflow (minutes)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Operates BI tools (Metabase, Jupyter) to analyze data, build dashboards, generate narratives/decks, and send proactive alerts.
- **Weaknesses:** Heavy reliance on browser-based automation (Chrome extension) for tool interoperability.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from a tool-specific assistant to a comprehensive "agentic BI" platform and research lab.
- **M&A:** none visible
