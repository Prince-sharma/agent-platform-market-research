# Sierra

**Thesis (one line):** Bret Taylor's enterprise agent platform for autonomous customer service; category leader scaling beyond support into long-horizon enterprise agents.

## Facts

- Founded: 2023 (Bret Taylor, Clay Bavor)
- HQ: San Francisco
- YC batch: none
- Backers: Tiger Global and GV (led $950M Series E, May 2026); Benchmark, Sequoia, ICONIQ, Thrive, Conviction, Greenoaks
- Funding: Over $1B total; $950M Series E (May 2026); prior round priced it at $10B (late 2025)
- Valuation: $15.8B post-money (May 2026); $10B (late 2025)
- Revenue/ARR: $200M annualized (May 2026); crossed $100M ARR within seven quarters of Feb 2024 launch
- ICP: Large enterprises, B2C brands (SiriusXM, Sonos, WeightWatchers, Brex)
- Pricing model: Outcome-based (per resolution); blended consumption pricing for routing/greeter tasks

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Hybrid; Agent Studio no-code canvas plus code-first Agent SDK.
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS.
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary connector library (40+ prebuilt) extensible via Agent SDK.
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Built-in RAG with live sync to systems of record; agent memory layer.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Fully autonomous agents with supervisory guardrail layers; intelligent handoff to human agents.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** SOC 2, HIPAA, GDPR, CCPA, PCI DSS Level 1, FedRAMP High, CSA STAR, ISO 27001, ISO 42001.
7. **Pricing model:** Outcome-based (per resolution); blended consumption pricing for routing/greeter tasks.
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led enterprise; lands via customer service, expands across functions.
9. **Moat thesis:** Enterprise trust and compliance depth, integration breadth, outcome-pricing alignment, founder distribution.
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary.
11. **Task horizon:** Long-horizon (days to weeks) via Horizon platform launched with Takeoff acquisition (Jul 2026).

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end resolution across channels: account actions, transaction processing, multi-step workflows (Brex reports 90% faster service).
- **Weaknesses:** Enterprise-only cost of entry; outcome-based contracts complex to negotiate; intensifying competition (Decagon, incumbent CX suites).
- **Trajectory:** From customer support into broader customer relationships, sales, and long-horizon autonomous enterprise agents.
- **M&A:** Receptive AI (Mar 2025), Opera Tech in Japan (Mar 2026), Fragment (Apr 2026), Takeoff (Jul 2026, with Horizon platform launch).
