# Writer

**Thesis (one line):** Full-stack enterprise generative AI platform providing proprietary Palmyra models and agentic workflows for on-brand, compliant corporate automation.

- Pricing model: Per-seat (Starter) and platform fee with optional solution packs (Enterprise)

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Natural language via WRITER Agent and a workflow engine for Playbooks
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary connector library (e.g., Salesforce, Snowflake, Adobe, Microsoft 365)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Built-in RAG via Knowledge Graph and live sync with systems of record
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Approval gates and guardrails; supports autonomous execution via Playbooks
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC, audit logs, SAML SSO, SCIM, and compliance certs (SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, GDPR)
7. **Pricing model:** Per seat for Starter; platform fee plus usage/solution packs for Enterprise
8. **GTM motion:** PLG self-serve (Starter) and sales-led (Enterprise)
9. **Moat thesis:** Vertical integration of proprietary LLMs (Palmyra), Knowledge Graph, and brand-specific DNA
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow (minutes) to persistent (always-on) routines

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Executes end-to-end GTM motions including campaign creation, sales personalization, account prep, and meeting intelligence.
- **Weaknesses:** Heavy reliance on proprietary ecosystem; limited transparency on specific model performance vs frontier open-weights.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from content generation to "agentic GTM" with a focus on autonomous, always-on revenue operations.
- **M&A:** none visible
