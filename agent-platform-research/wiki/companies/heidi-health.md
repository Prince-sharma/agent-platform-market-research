# Heidi Health

**Thesis (one line):** AI-powered clinical documentation and evidence platform providing medical scribing, agentic administrative tasks, and citation-backed clinical decision support for clinicians.

## Facts

- Founded: unknown
- HQ: unknown
- YC batch: none
- Backers: Point72 (lead)
- Funding: $65M Series B (Oct 2025)
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Individual clinicians, medical practices, and large healthcare organizations
- Pricing model: Tiered subscription (Free, Clinician, Practice, Enterprise)

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Natural language (Ask Heidi) and template-based configuration
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** EHR integrations (Embedded and Connected)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Built-in RAG (Heidi Evidence) with transparent citations and preferred sources/journals
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Approval gates (clinician review of notes); agentic actions for exports, emails, and PDFs
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC (team management), SSO, flexible data retention, and healthcare-grade security
7. **Pricing model:** Per seat/tier; usage-based "credits" for advanced actions on Free plan
8. **GTM motion:** PLG self-serve (Free tier) and sales-led (Enterprise)
9. **Moat thesis:** Workflow lock-in via EHR integration and proprietary clinical evidence library
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow (transcription to note generation to EHR export)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end clinical documentation including ambient transcription, custom template application, "Ask Heidi" for note editing, and agentic administrative tasks (emails, PDFs, PPTs). Includes a specialized "Evidence" agent for citation-backed medical research.
- **Weaknesses:** Regional feature disparity (e.g., in-session Evidence limitations in EU/UK).
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from a specialized scribe tool to a comprehensive "AI Care Partner" integrating clinical evidence and administrative automation.
- **M&A:** None visible
