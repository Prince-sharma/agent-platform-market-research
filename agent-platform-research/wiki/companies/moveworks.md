# Moveworks

**Thesis (one line):** An enterprise agentic AI platform resolving IT, HR, and finance requests, now serving as a critical agent layer for ServiceNow.

## Facts

- Founded: unknown
- HQ: Mountain View, CA
- YC batch: none
- Backers: Tiger Global (lead), Alkeon Capital (lead), Lightspeed Venture Partners, Sapphire Ventures, Bain Capital, ICONIQ Growth, Kleiner Perkins
- Funding: $315M total; $200M Series C (2021)
- Valuation: $2.1B (2021)
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Large enterprises (350+ companies, including 10% of Fortune 500)
- Pricing model: Custom quote

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** No-code "Assistant Builder" and "Agent Studio" (including a low-code compound action editor and YAML-based pro-code syntax).
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS.
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary connectors for platforms including ServiceNow, Salesforce, Slack, and Microsoft SharePoint; includes an AI Agent Marketplace.
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Built-in RAG via next-generation Enterprise Search providing a unified interface across applications and documents.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Fully autonomous resolution (resolving 3 in 4 issues autonomously for some clients) with "blocking actions" in conversational processes for reliability.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC controls and a Data Governance Layer for sensitive data and analytics.
7. **Pricing model:** Custom enterprise pricing (unknown specific unit).
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led.
9. **Moat thesis:** Integration depth with enterprise systems of record and distribution via ServiceNow acquisition.
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary.
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow (minutes) via "Ambient Agents" and "Compound Actions."

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Agents autonomously resolve employee issues across IT, HR, and Finance. Capabilities include specialized "Scoped Assistants" (e.g., Prospecting Coach, Recruiting Assistant) and a reasoning engine utilizing models like GPT-5.1 and Gemini 3 Pro/Flash for complex tool use.
- **Weaknesses:** Squeezed by large suites (leading to its acquisition) and well-funded independents like Resolve.
- **Trajectory:** Transitioning from a standalone vertical agent to an integrated agentic layer within the ServiceNow ecosystem.
- **M&A:** Acquired by ServiceNow for $2.85B in cash and stock (announced March 10, 2025).
