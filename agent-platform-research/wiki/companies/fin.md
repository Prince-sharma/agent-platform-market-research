# Fin

**Thesis (one line):** Formerly Intercom, Fin is a customer service suite that rebranded to signal a total pivot to an AI-agent-first business model.

## Facts

- Founded: 2011
- HQ: San Francisco
- YC batch: none
- Backers: Bessemer Venture Partners (lead), Index Ventures, Kleiner Perkins, Google Ventures
- Funding: $250M venture debt (March 2026); previous rounds include Series D ($125M)
- Valuation: $3.6B (agreed acquisition price June 2026)
- Revenue/ARR: $400M total ARR (2026); Fin agent nearing $100M ARR (August 2026)
- ICP: Customer service and support operations teams
- Pricing model: Outcome-based ($0.99 per resolved conversation)

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** natural language
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** proprietary connector library
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** built-in RAG
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** approval gates; guardrails
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC, audit trails, compliance certs
7. **Pricing model:** per outcome
8. **GTM motion:** sales-led
9. **Moat thesis:** distribution, workflow lock-in
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** multi-step workflow (minutes)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end customer resolution across service, sales, and ecommerce; includes "Fin Operator" for back-office agent management.
- **Weaknesses:** Significant legacy baggage from the Intercom suite transition.
- **Trajectory:** Moving toward a "single agent" for the entire customer journey; currently being acquired by Salesforce.
- **M&A:** Agreed to be acquired by Salesforce in June 2026 for ~$3.6B.
