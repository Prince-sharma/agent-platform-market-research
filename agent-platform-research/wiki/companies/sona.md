# Sona

**Thesis (one line):** An agentic AI platform managing frontline workforce operations, replacing legacy scheduling and HR tools with an autonomous operational foundation.

## Facts

- Founded: unknown
- HQ: London
- YC batch: none
- Backers: N47 (lead), Felicis, Northzone, Gradient, Italian Founders Fund
- Funding: >$100M total; $45M Series B (Apr 2026)
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Large frontline enterprises in hospitality, retail, healthcare, and logistics
- Pricing model: Custom quotes based on business size and sector

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Natural language and AI-driven (via "Forge" enterprise AI application builder for custom software).
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS.
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary integrations with operational data (bookings, revenue, weather) and core systems (payroll, HR).
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Live sync with systems of record; acts as the primary system of record for scheduling, HR, payroll, and BI.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Hybrid; provides AI assistant "Raffy" for manager guidance and autonomous forecasting/scheduling.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** RBAC and compliance (includes "regulated workforce infrastructure").
7. **Pricing model:** Custom/Tailored (based on ROI estimates and organization size).
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led (requires consultation and tailored demos).
9. **Moat thesis:** Data gravity (primary system of record) and workflow lock-in via the "Forge" custom application layer.
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary.
11. **Task horizon:** Persistent (always-on operational foundation for workforce management).

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Agents handle end-to-end workforce management: predicting labor demand using real-time external data (weather, road closures), automating scheduling, and managing payroll/compliance.
- **Weaknesses:** High dependency on custom sales cycles and implementation for enterprise-level complexity.
- **Trajectory:** Moving from a vertical WFM tool to a horizontal "operational foundation" where businesses build their own custom agentic apps via Forge.
- **M&A:** None visible.
