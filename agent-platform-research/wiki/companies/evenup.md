# EvenUp

**Thesis (one line):** AI-native claims intelligence platform automating demand packages and pre-litigation workflows for personal injury law firms.

## Facts

- Founded: unknown
- HQ: unknown
- YC batch: none
- Backers: Bessemer Venture Partners (lead), BVP
- Funding: $150M Series E (Oct 2025); total unknown
- Valuation: $2B+ (Oct 2025)
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Personal injury plaintiff law firms
- Pricing model: Custom; case-based pricing and per-user subscriptions (est. $200-$500/user/mo)

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Natural language and smart workflows via AI Assistant and AI Drafts.
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS.
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary connector library to sync with existing case management software.
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Built-in RAG powered by Piai, trained on hundreds of thousands of injury cases and millions of medical visits.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Approval gates; provides "Expert Demands" with professional review and AI-drafted documents for attorney review.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** unknown
7. **Pricing model:** Hybrid: per-case pricing and per-user subscription.
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led (requires demo/call for custom pricing).
9. **Moat thesis:** Data gravity via Piai (largest PI dataset) and deep vertical workflow lock-in.
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary.
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow (minutes to days) covering intake, treatment, and demand drafting.

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end pre-litigation automation including medical record review, chronology generation (MedChrons), demand drafting, and client communication via voice/text agents.
- **Weaknesses:** High reliance on custom sales cycles and potential friction in onboarding with legacy case management systems.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from a drafting tool to a full "Pre-Litigation as a Service" (PLAAS) platform covering the entire case lifecycle.
- **M&A:** none visible
