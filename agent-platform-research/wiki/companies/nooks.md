# Nooks

**Thesis (one line):** An AI-native sales engagement platform specializing in parallel dialing and agentic sequencing to maximize SDR pipeline efficiency.

- Pricing model: Custom quotes; estimated $4-5K/User/Year (2026)

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Natural language AI chat interface for account planning and a dedicated dialer workspace with live battlecards.
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Vendor multi-tenant SaaS.
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Proprietary connector library integrating with Salesforce, HubSpot, Outreach, Salesloft, Gong, Apollo, and Clay.
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Live sync with CRM; integrated enrichment via Wiza, People Data Labs, Apollo, Forager, Datagma, and Prospeo.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Human-in-the-loop; AI detects live pickups and provides real-time transcription/battlecards for the human rep.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** Bi-directional CRM sync for activity logging; carrier registration and reputation monitoring for spam protection.
7. **Pricing model:** Per seat (estimated $4-5K/user/year).
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led (custom quotes).
9. **Moat thesis:** Workflow lock-in via parallel dialing efficiency and deep integration into the SDR's daily calling habit.
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary.
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow (minutes to hours) covering prospecting, sequencing, and live calling.

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Automates the "top of funnel" by parallel dialing multiple leads, detecting live answers, and providing real-time AI coaching/scripts.
- **Weaknesses:** Heavy reliance on third-party data providers for contact information; high dependency on human reps for the final conversion.
- **Trajectory:** Expanding from a specialized dialer into a full AI Sales Engagement Platform replacing legacy sequencers like Outreach and Salesloft.
- **M&A:** none visible
