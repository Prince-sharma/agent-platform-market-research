# Capgemini

**Thesis (one line):** Global SI providing a productized agentic AI foundation and industry-specific suites to scale autonomous AI across enterprise ecosystems.

- YC batch: none

- Pricing model: unknown

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** Natural language and config workflows via agentic gallery and RAISE foundation; or unknown
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** Flexible; supports hyperscalers, on-premises, or hybrid environments
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** Extensive partner network (AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft, Databricks, Informatica)
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Live sync with systems of record; domain-aware agents with natural language to SQL
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Autonomous orchestration with built-in safeguards, governance, and ethical AI guardrails
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** Full governance, security, monitoring, and responsible AI guardrails via RAISE
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** Services-led; productized accelerators (RAISE) and industry suites (BI Suite) to move from PoC to scale
9. **Moat thesis:** Distribution via global SI footprint and deep industry-specific domain expertise
10. **Open-source posture:** Proprietary foundation; open ecosystem with no single-vendor lock-in
11. **Task horizon:** Multi-step workflow to persistent (autonomous BI lifecycle orchestration)

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** Automates end-to-end BI lifecycles (reporting, monitoring, distribution) and bespoke industry processes via custom or off-the-shelf agents.
- **Weaknesses:** Heavy reliance on professional services for deployment; lack of transparent, self-serve commercial models.
- **Trajectory:** Shifting from pure consulting to "Intelligence-as-a-Service" using productized frameworks like Resonance and RAISE.
- **M&A:** none visible
