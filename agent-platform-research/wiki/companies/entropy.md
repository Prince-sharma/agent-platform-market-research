# Entropy

**Thesis (one line):** An AI operating system for specialty clinics automating patient intake and revenue cycle management via a provider-level context layer.

## Facts

- Founded: 2024
- HQ: San Francisco
- YC batch: S24
- Backers: Andreessen Horowitz (lead)
- Funding: $25M seed
- Valuation: unknown
- Revenue/ARR: unknown
- ICP: Specialty healthcare clinics
- Pricing model: unknown

## The eleven build decisions

1. **Build interface:** unknown
2. **Runtime and tenancy:** vendor multi-tenant SaaS
3. **Tool and integration strategy:** APIs, browser agents, and voice agents to connect with any EHR, billing platform, or payor portal.
4. **Knowledge and data strategy:** Built-in RAG via "Atlas," a provider-level context layer that captures and evolves institutional knowledge.
5. **Autonomy and human-in-the-loop:** Human-in-the-loop workflows designed for clinical grade accuracy.
6. **Governance and enterprise controls:** HIPAA and SOC 2 Type 2 compliant.
7. **Pricing model:** unknown
8. **GTM motion:** Sales-led (demo-driven with AI audits).
9. **Moat thesis:** Data gravity and workflow lock-in via the Atlas context layer, which adapts to changing payor requirements autonomously.
10. **Open-source posture:** proprietary
11. **Task horizon:** multi-step workflow (minutes to hours) covering intake, eligibility, and denial management.

## Analysis

- **Agent capability depth:** End-to-end automation of the referral and revenue cycle: triaging faxes/emails, verifying insurance eligibility, submitting prior authorizations, and challenging denied claims.
- **Weaknesses:** High dependency on the reliability of browser agents for legacy EHRs/portals that lack APIs.
- **Trajectory:** Moving toward a comprehensive "Operating System" for specialty healthcare by expanding the Atlas context layer to cover more clinical operations.
- **M&A:** none visible
