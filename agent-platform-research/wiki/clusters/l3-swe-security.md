# L3 Software Engineering and Security Agents

## Scope

89 companies profiled in this cluster during Phase 2 (assignment file: data/phase2-assignments/p2-3e-swe-security.txt).

## Notable companies

- Cursor (Anysphere): AI-assisted IDE with YOLO mode for autonomous iteration; over $500M ARR and $9.9B valuation.
- Cognition (Devin): Autonomous AI software engineer; $10.2B valuation (2025) and acquirer of Windsurf.
- Replit: AI agent platform for natural language software building; $9B valuation following a $400M round in 2026.
- Windsurf: Agentic AI code editor and enterprise platform; acquired by Cognition in 2025.
- Horizon3.ai: Autonomous penetration testing via NodeZero; $2B+ valuation as of August 2026.
- CodeRabbit: Autonomous AI code review and change management; $1.5B valuation (2026).
- Blitzy: End-to-end autonomous enterprise software generation; $1.4B valuation (May 2026).
- Augment Code: Codebase-aware enterprise agents; ~$20M ARR (2025) and $252M raised.
- XBOW: Autonomous offensive security pentesting agent; $120M Series C (March 2026).
- 7ai: Autonomous agents for security operations; $130M Series A (December 2025).

## Patterns across the eleven build decisions

Convergence is strongest in the build interface, where IDE/editors (13) and web platforms (7) dominate. GTM motions lean toward PLG/self-serve (15) over sales-led (7). In terms of autonomy, there is a clear split between fully autonomous agents (15) and those utilizing approval gates (6). Task horizons are primarily multi-step/minutes (19), though a significant minority handle long-horizon tasks of hours or more (5).

Divergence is noted in the open-source posture, with a divide between proprietary models (20) and OSS cores (8). Pricing is fragmented across usage-based (7), per-seat (6), and custom enterprise (4) models.

Unknown rates are high across all categories (ranging from 60 to 69), reflecting the high volume of seed-stage companies profiled at base depth.

## Consolidation and M&A

The cluster is seeing active consolidation of agentic tooling:
- Cognition (Devin) acquired Windsurf on 2025-07-14, integrating an agentic IDE into its autonomous engineer offering.
- CodeRabbit acquired FluxNinja.
- Windsurf's history includes a $2.4 billion talent/licensing deal with Google prior to its acquisition by Cognition.

## Traction distribution

The cluster exhibits a heavy-top distribution:
- Unicorns: 7 companies ($1B+ valuation), including Cursor, Cognition, Replit, and Horizon3.ai.
- Late-stage/High-funding: 10 companies with $100M+ raised, including Factory, Exaforce, and Sourcegraph (Amp).
- Mid-tier: 23 companies with $10M-$100M raised.
- Seed/Early: 2 companies under $10M and 47 companies with no visible traction.

## Gaps and open ground

There is significant uncertainty regarding runtime and tenancy, with 65 companies listing this as unknown and only 3 opting for self-host/local deployments. The "Knowledge and data strategy" is thinly defined, with only 7 companies explicitly citing RAG/code context. Additionally, the "Engineering" vertical is small (6 companies) compared to "Software Engineering" (63), suggesting a potential gap in agents targeting broader hardware or systems engineering.
